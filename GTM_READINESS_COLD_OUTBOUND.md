## Verdict

**Start cold outbound in Month 2 — but only after the blockers below are cleared.**

Do not launch cold outreach before the hard blockers are resolved. But do not wait for warm network to "run out" either. The warm network is the asset that makes the first case studies possible. Cold outbound and warm outreach run in parallel, not sequence.

The assumption that "website + case study = ready for cold outbound" is **dangerously incomplete**. Here is why, and what actually needs to exist.

## Hard Blockers — Cold Outbound Does NOT Start Without These

These are binary conditions. If any is missing, cold outbound does not begin.

### BLOCKER 1: Live website at alize.studio with a functional diagnostic CTA

**What "live" means:**
- Domain resolves and loads without errors
- Hero section clearly states what Alizé does, who it's for, and what to do next
- "Book a diagnostic" or equivalent CTA exists and is functional
- Pricing or offer structure is visible (even if "starting at €X/month")
- At minimum: one page, no 404s, no placeholder text

**Why this is a blocker:**
Every cold outreach recipient will visit your website before responding — if they respond at all. If they land on a 404, a Coming Soon page, or a generic "we build AI agents" page with no offer, you are disqualified before the conversation starts.

**What does NOT count:** A Notion doc, a shared Figma link, a Landing page on a subdomain. It needs to be the real domain, live, with real content.

**BLOCKER STATUS:** Domain not bought yet. This is the first blocker and it is not cleared.

---

### BLOCKER 2: At least ONE completed pilot with documented results

**What "completed" means:**
- Agent deployed at a real client
- Real business workflow automated (not a demo or internal test)
- Measurable outcome documented: tasks handled, time saved, error reduction, or operational ownership transferred
- Client permission to use as a reference (anonymized is acceptable for cold outreach, but the case must be real)

**Why this is a blocker:**
Cold outbound to PME/ETI decision-makers requires social proof. "We build AI agents for businesses like yours" means nothing without evidence. One real case study with real numbers is worth more than three generic claims.

The BRIEF says pilots start in Month 1. If the first pilot is not completed before Month 2 cold outreach begins, you are outreach before you have anything to defend.

**What does NOT count:** Internal testing. A demo you ran for yourself. A proof-of-concept built on sample data. A case study from a previous job that Alizé didn't deliver.

**BLOCKER STATUS:** No pilot completed. This is Month 1 work that must finish before Month 2 cold outreach begins.

### BLOCKER 3: Confirmed ICP — which exact type of company are you calling?

**What "confirmed" means:**
- You can name the exact segment (e.g., "professional services firms, 20-50 employees, Île-de-France, using 3+ business tools")
- You have a list of 100+ target companies that match this description
- You know who the decision-maker title is (Founder, COO, DGA)
- You know what pain you are calling about (e.g., "your team spends 3+ hours/week on request routing")

**Why this is a blocker:**
Cold outreach sent to the wrong ICP produces no pipeline, corrupts your learning data, and wastes the first months of outreach on companies that will never buy. The ICP defined in the BRIEF (PME/ETI 50-200 employees, professional services) is a starting point — it must be narrowed and confirmed before cold outreach begins.

The unresolved TODO item U12 (first vertical selection) must be resolved before cold outbound begins. You cannot sell to "French PME" generically.

**BLOCKER STATUS:** ICP partially defined but not locked. U12 is unresolved.

## Required Assets — Must Exist, But Can Be Incomplete

These must exist in some form. They can be rough, but they must exist.

### ASSET 1: Outreach messaging — problem-first, not product-first

**What this means:**
- A cold email template that opens with a specific business problem, not "AI agents" or "automation"
- No mention of competitors
- No technical jargon (no MCP, no RAG, no LLM)
- One clear CTA (book a call, get a diagnostic)
- Written in French, naturally

**Example of the right opening:**
> "Bonjour [Name], J'ai vu que [Company] grandit vite sur [specific channel]. Ce qui suit n'est pas pour vous si votre équipe a déjà 2h/jour de libres sur les tâches admin — sinon, on peut parler."

**Example of the wrong opening:**
> "Nous offrons des agents IA gérés pour votre entreprise avec une stack technique MCP-native..."

**Why required:** Without tested messaging, the first cold campaign will read like every other AI vendor spam. PME decision-makers receive 10+ AI pitches per week. Yours must stand out by leading with their problem, not your technology.

**Nice to have:** A/B variants. Multi-sequence follow-up cadence. LinkedIn InMail variants.

---

### ASSET 2: LinkedIn presence — company page + personal founder profile active

**What this means:**
- Alizé company page exists on LinkedIn with basic info (description, website link, logo)
- Louis's personal LinkedIn has a profile that references Alizé and his focus on AI agents for PME
- At minimum 3-5 posts or articles published (can be short, can be thought leadership, must be real)

**Why required:** Every cold prospect will check your LinkedIn. If Alizé has 0 posts, 0 followers, and no company page, the brand looks like a ghost operation. If Louis's LinkedIn doesn't reference what he's building, prospects can't connect the email to a real person.

**Nice to have:** High post engagement. A large follower count. Professional profile photography.

---

### ASSET 3: One-pager or leave-behind document

**What this means:**
- A PDF (1-2 pages) that explains: what Alizé does, who it's for, how it works, pricing structure, and a proof point
- Can be a structured email, a Notion page exported as PDF, or a simple layout
- Does not need to be designed to agency standards — needs to be real and accurate

**Why required:** Cold prospects who don't respond to email often don't respond because they had no immediate reason to. A leave-behind that lands in their inbox as a follow-up creates a second touchpoint with substance.

**Nice to have:** Custom-designed layout. Sector-specific variants. ROI calculator.

---

### ASSET 4: Pricing structure finalized and visible

**What this means:**
- Pilot price: €4,500 fixed all-in (per D24)
- Managed service: €1,200-1,500/month starter / €2,000-2,500/month multi-workflow (per D43)
- These prices are written somewhere accessible (website, one-pager, or proposal template)

**Why required:** Cold outreach that generates a discovery call will immediately face "what does it cost?" If you don't have prices, you either delay (momentum killer) or give a vague answer (credibility killer). Prices don't need to be public, but you must have them ready for the call.

**Nice to have:** Published pricing page. ROI calculator. Payment terms documented.

---

### ASSET 5: Technical execution path confirmed

**What this means:**
- Either n8n is set up and tested for at least one workflow type
- OR Louis has a documented manual execution path for the first pilot
- At least one workflow has been demonstrated working end-to-end

**Why required:** Cold outreach that converts to a signed pilot will expect a delivery plan in the first meeting. If you cannot show even a rough technical path, the prospect will sense that you're not ready.

**Nice to have:** Full MCP integration. Production-grade infrastructure. Multi-tenant setup.

## The Warm Network Question

**Should warm network be exhausted before cold outbound starts?**

No. This is the wrong framing. Here's why:

1. **Warm and cold run in parallel, not sequence.** Month 1 warm outreach should happen simultaneously as you build cold-outbound assets. They are not sequential phases.

2. **Warm network has a natural ceiling.** If Louis has 50-75 warm contacts, and maybe 10-20% convert to discovery calls, that's 5-15 discovery calls from warm network in Month 1. That's enough for 1-3 pilots. It is not enough for 6-8 clients.

3. **Cold outreach builds while warm network is active.** The cold pipeline takes 4-6 weeks to produce meetings from a cold campaign. If you wait until warm network is "exhausted," you'll have a gap between warm pipeline drying up and cold pipeline ramping up.

4. **Warm network generates the proof that makes cold outreach credible.** The first 2-3 case studies should come from warm network. Those case studies are what make cold outreach viable. Do not start cold outreach until you have at least one real case study from warm network.

**The correct sequence:**
- Month 1: Warm outreach (personal network) → Book discovery calls → Close 1-2 pilots → Deliver pilots → Generate case studies
- Month 1 (concurrently): Build cold-outbound assets (website, messaging, ICP list, technical setup)
- Month 2: Start cold outreach only after: website live + one case study exists + ICP locked + delivery confirmed
- Month 2 (concurrently): Continue warm outreach — it does not stop

## Bottom Line

**Do not cold outbound without:**
1. A live website that converts
2. At least one real case study
3. A confirmed ICP with a target list
4. A confirmed delivery path

**Do not wait to cold outbound until:**
- Warm network is exhausted (it will create a pipeline gap)
- Perfect case studies exist (you need 1, not 3)
- All nice-to-haves are built (they are enhancements, not prerequisites)

**The warm network and cold outbound are not sequential.** Both run from Month 1 onward. Warm generates the proof. Cold scales the pipeline. They feed each other.

Start cold outreach in Month 2 — after the four blockers are cleared, and after at least one case study exists from warm network delivery. Not before.
