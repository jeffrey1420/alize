## Executive Summary

This document defines Alizé's go-to-market strategy for acquiring French PME/ETI customers. The GTM combines outbound prospecting, inbound marketing, strategic partnerships, and a structured sales process to achieve €540K ARR in Year 1 with <10% monthly churn.

---

## Market Thesis

**Core Insight:** French PME/ETI (20-200 employees) are underserved by AI agent platforms. They face:
- Language barriers with English-first AI tools
- Complexity overwhelming their limited IT resources
- Data sovereignty concerns that block US-based solutions
- Need for hand-holding through adoption
- Desire for predictable costs, not usage-based pricing

**Alizé's Positioning:** The AI agent service that "speaks French, stays in France, and delivers in 14 days."

## Pricing Strategy

### Pricing Philosophy

1. **Predictable > Usage-based** — French companies prefer budget certainty
2. **Value-based** — Price based on ROI delivered, not technology cost
3. **Transparent** — Public pricing page, no hidden fees
4. **Anchored** — Start with pilot at known price, transition to retainer

### Pricing Tiers

#### Starter — €500/month
- **Ideal for:** Early-stage PME, single-department automation
- **Agents:** Up to 3 concurrent agents
- **Tool Integrations:** Email, calendar, documents (10GB)
- **API Calls:** 10,000/month
- **Support:** Email (48h response)
- **SLA:** 99% uptime
- **Contract:** Month-to-month

#### Professional — €1,000/month
- **Ideal for:** Growing PME with cross-function needs
- **Agents:** Up to 10 concurrent agents
- **Tool Integrations:** + CRM (HubSpot), accounting software
- **API Calls:** 50,000/month
- **Support:** Email + phone (24h response)
- **SLA:** 99.5% uptime
- **Contract:** 6-month minimum

#### Enterprise — €2,000/month
- **Ideal for:** ETI with enterprise integration needs
- **Agents:** Up to 30 concurrent agents
- **Tool Integrations:** Full suite (ERP, CRM, custom APIs)
- **API Calls:** Unlimited
- **Support:** Dedicated account manager
- **SLA:** 99.9% uptime, 4h escalation
- **Contract:** 12-month minimum

### Pilot Program Pricing

**Purpose:** Lower the barrier to trial, demonstrate value quickly

- **Duration:** 30 days
- **Price:** €200 (credited toward Professional plan)
- **Scope:** 1 department, 1 use case, 1 agent type
- **Deliverables:** Live agent, daily metrics, ROI report
- **Success Criteria:** Pre-agreed KPI improvement (e.g., "reduce invoice processing from 45min to 10min")
- **Conversion:** 80%+ pilot-to-retainer target

### Annual Discount

- Pay annually: 2 months free (effectively 14% discount)
- Pay quarterly: 1 month free (8% discount)

## Sales Process

### Stage 1: Prospecting & Qualification (Week 1-2)

**Activities:**
1. Identify target companies via LinkedIn Sales Navigator
2. Filter by: Industry, size, digital maturity signals, hiring patterns
3. Initial outreach via LinkedIn + email
4. Cold call to set discovery meeting

**Qualification Criteria (BANT-ish):**
- Budget: €500-3000/month authority exists
- Authority: Decision maker reachable
- Need: Identified pain point (admin burden, efficiency gap)
- Timeline: Active problem, willing to engage in 4-8 weeks

**Qualified Lead Definition:**
- 20-200 employees in target industry
- Identified pain point
- Decision maker willing to meet
- Timeline < 3 months

### Stage 2: Discovery Meeting (Week 2-3)

**Format:** 30-minute video call or in-person

**Agenda:**
1. 5 min: Introduction, confirm fit
2. 10 min: Understand current state, pain points
3. 10 min: Present Alizé approach, relevant case study
4. 5 min: Propose next steps (diagnostic/pilot)

**Discovery Questions:**
- "Quelles sont les 3 tâches qui prennent le plus de temps à votre équipe ?"
- "Si vous pouviez automatiser une chose demain, quelle serait-elle ?"
- "Combien d'heures par semaine sont consacrées à [identified task]?"
- "Quel est votre budget pour résoudre ce problème ?"
- "Qui d'autre serait impliqué dans cette décision ?"

**Output:** Qualified opportunity with identified use case

### Stage 3: Diagnostic (Week 3-4)

**Purpose:** Deep dive into the specific use case, quantify the opportunity

**Activities:**
1. 1-hour workshop with key stakeholders
2. Current state process mapping
3. Data gathering (volume, frequency, current tools)
4. ROI estimation (time saved, error reduction, scale enabled)

**Diagnostic Deliverable:** "Diagnostic Report" with:
- Current state analysis
- Proposed solution approach
- Quantified ROI (hours saved, cost reduction)
- Recommended pilot scope

**Output:** Signed pilot agreement or clear disqualification

### Stage 4: Pilot Agreement (Week 4)

**Activities:**
1. Pilot terms negotiation (scope, price, timeline)
2. Legal/contract review
3. Signature (DocuSign)
4. Kickoff meeting

**Pilot Contract Elements:**
- 30-day pilot period
- €200 pilot fee (credited to retainer)
- Success metrics (agreed-upon KPIs)
- Data access permissions
- Transition to retainer terms

**Output:** Signed pilot contract, kickoff scheduled

### Stage 5: Pilot Execution (Week 1-4 of pilot)

**Week 1: Setup**
- Technical integration (email, calendar, tools)
- Agent configuration based on diagnostic
- Test queries and validation

**Week 2: Training**
- Train agent on company-specific context
- Fine-tune prompts with real examples
- User acceptance testing with champion

**Week 3: Go-Live**
- Deploy agent to production
- User training and documentation
- Daily check-ins with champion

**Week 4: Evaluation**
- Collect usage metrics and feedback
- Compare to baseline KPIs
- Document pilot results

**Output:** Pilot report with ROI documentation, conversion recommendation

### Stage 6: Retainer Conversion (Week 4-5)

**Activities:**
1. Present pilot results to decision-maker
2. Propose retainer plan (Starter/Professional/Enterprise)
3. Negotiate terms if needed
4. Sign annual contract
5. Transition to customer success onboarding

**Conversion Criteria:**
- Pilot success (KPI improvement achieved)
- Champion satisfied and advocates internally
- Decision-maker sees value
- Budget available

**Output:** Signed retainer contract, transition to CS
