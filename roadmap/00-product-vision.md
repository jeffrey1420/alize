# Alizé Product Vision & Positioning Roadmap

**Version:** 1.0  
**Last Updated:** 2026-03-30  
**Status:** Foundation Document

---

## Executive Summary

Alizé is a managed AI agent service designed specifically for French PME (20-50 employees) and ETI (50-200 employees) companies. We provide turnkey AI agents that diagnose business needs, run targeted pilots, and transition into monthly retainer engagements—all with French-native support and data sovereignty guarantees.

---

## Vision Statement

**"Alizé empowers French PME/ETI to harness AI agents as a trusted operational partner—not a toy, not a threat, but a competitive advantage that works in French, thinks French, and understands French business culture."**

---

## Strategic Pillars

1. **Simplicity First** — From diagnostic to deployed agent in 14 days
2. **French-Native** — All agents speak fluent French, understand French business norms
3. **Data Sovereignty** — All data stays in France/Germany/EU, GDPR-compliant by default
4. **Managed Service** — We build it, run it, improve it—you just validate results
5. **Measurable ROI** — Every engagement tied to concrete business KPIs

## Competitive Positioning

### Alizé vs. Generic AI Platforms (ChatGPT Enterprise, Agentova)

| Dimension | Generic Platforms | Alizé |
|-----------|-------------------|-------|
| Language | English-first, French OK | French-native, native idioms |
| Setup Time | Weeks of configuration | 14-day diagnostic to deploy |
| Support | Ticket system, documentation | Dedicated French-speaking team |
| Customization | Generic agents | Industry-specific agents |
| Data Location | US or ambiguous | France/Germany guaranteed |
| Pricing Model | Per-token or per-seat | Predictable monthly retainer |
| Onboarding | Self-serve | Managed white-glove service |

### Differentiators

1. **"AI that speaks French business"** — Agents trained on French business correspondence, legal documents, and cultural context
2. **14-day velocity** — From first call to live agent in 2 weeks
3. **Managed risk** — We guarantee uptime, accuracy, and continuous improvement
4. **Vertical depth** — Pre-built agents for French accounting, HR, legal, and customer service
5. **EU data sovereignty** — No US data transfers, full GDPR Article 44+ compliance

## Target Customer Profile

### Primary: PME (20-50 employees)
- **Characteristics:** Family-owned or founder-led, limited IT staff, growing pressure to adopt AI
- **Pain Points:** No time to configure AI, fear of complexity, data privacy concerns
- **Typical Departments:** Administration, Commercial, RH
- **Decision Maker:** Gérant / PDG / Dirigeant
- **Budget Authority:** €500-1500/month retainer
- **Purchase Trigger:** Competitor using AI, employee burnout, growth plateau

### Secondary: ETI (50-200 employees)
- **Characteristics:** Professional management, dedicated department heads, IT coordinator
- **Pain Points:** Need coordination across departments, require SLA guarantees, need ROI proof
- **Typical Departments:** All core business functions
- **Decision Maker:** DSI / Directeur Général / Directeur de Site
- **Budget Authority:** €1500-3000/month retainer
- **Purchase Trigger:** Cost reduction mandate,数字化转型 initiative, AI pilot failure elsewhere

## Brand Identity Guidelines

### Positioning Statement

"For French PME and ETI companies seeking AI agents that work as hard as they do—Alizé is the managed AI service that speaks French, stays in Europe, and delivers measurable results in 14 days."

### Brand Personality

- **Confident but accessible** — Expert-level AI, not intimidating
- **Reliable like a good craftsman** — We show up, we deliver, we follow through
- **Modern but rooted** — French business values, cutting-edge technology
- **Transparent** — Clear pricing, clear process, clear results

### Brand Voice

- Write as you speak: clear, direct, no buzzwords
- Always in French unless client prefers English
- Use "vous" consistently for B2B context
- Show numbers and proof, not promises

## Market Opportunity

### TAM (Total Addressable Market)

- France: ~35,000 PME (20-50 emp) + ~8,000 ETI (50-200 emp)
- Average retainer: €1,200/month PME, €2,400/month ETI
- Potential market size: €600M+/year

### SAM (Serviceable Addressable Market)

- Initial focus: Île-de-France + major métropoles (Lyon, Marseille, Toulouse, Bordeaux, Nantes, Lille)
- ~15,000 target companies in Phase 1 regions
- Potential: €180M/year

### SOM (Serviceable Obtainable Market)

- Year 1: 30 clients × €1,500/month = €540K ARR
- Year 2: 120 clients × €1,800/month = €2.6M ARR
- Year 3: 350 clients × €2,000/month = €8.4M ARR
