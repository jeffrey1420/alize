# AI Exit Readiness — Alizé for PE-Backed Companies

## The Problem

Private equity firms conducting due diligence on portfolio companies increasingly ask:

- Which AI agents does the company deploy, and on whose behalf?
- What decisions has AI made autonomously in the past 12 months?
- Can you demonstrate human oversight on AI-influenced outcomes?
- What is your audit trail if an AI decision is challenged post-acquisition?

**Answer most get:** crickets, or a hand-wavy "our team reviews everything."

**Answer acquirers want:** a governance layer with timestamps, human approvals, and tool-level audit logs.

## How Alizé Solves It

Alizé provides a managed AI agent governance layer for French PME that delivers exactly the audit trail due diligence requires:

| Due Diligence Question | Alizé Answer |
|---|---|
| What AI agents are active? | Central registry of all deployed agents with capability scope |

## Why It Matters for Exit

AI governance gaps are increasingly material to M&A risk:

1. **Liability transfer** — Acquirers inherit AI decisions made before closing. Without audit trails, they inherit unknown liability.
2. **EU AI Act** — High-risk AI systems under the Act require conformity assessments. Alizé's documentation layer is the foundation.
3. **Representations & Warranties** — AI-related reps & warranties are becoming standard in tech M&A. Alizé's audit log is the evidence package.
4. **Post-closing integration** — AI agent portfolios with governance can be integrated faster; unknowns slow deals.
