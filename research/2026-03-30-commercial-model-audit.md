After 45+ pulses and 240+ decisions, Alizé's commercial model is in structural collapse. Two of those three paths have since been killed: D232 killed downmarket, D224 killed channel partners. The remaining path — commercial co-founder — has zero market validation and no named candidate. ## Challenge 1: D219's Three Paths Have Collapsed to One — And That One Has Zero Validation

### Assumption Being Challenged
D219 declared the commercial model "structurally incompatible with Louis's credentials at €2,000+/month" and proposed three paths: commercial co-founder (A), downmarket (B), channel partner (C).

### Why the Assumption Is Wrong or Incomplete

The three-path framework has silently collapsed:

| Path | Decision | Status |
|------|----------|--------|
| A: Commercial co-founder | D219 | Active — no named candidate |
| B: Downmarket €500-800/mo | D232 | **KILLED** — unit economics fail |
| C: Channel partner | D224 | **KILLED** — Year 2+ only |

**The framework that was supposed to have three options now has one.** That one (commercial co-founder) has:
- No named candidate
- No timeline to find one
- No evidence that Louis can close a co-founder who can close
- Zero market validation that such a person exists in Louis's network or budget

D219 called this "structural incompatibility" — but the pulse process treated it as a solvable problem (find a commercial co-founder) rather than a structural constraint (Louis may not be fundable, investable, or partnerable at this stage regardless of who the commercial person is).

### What This Means

The pulse process killed two options without validating the third. This is not strategic narrowing — it's sequential elimination without information. The remaining path has no more evidence than the killed paths did when they were alive.

## Challenge 2: "Structurally Incompatible" Is Premature Without Any Market Data

### Assumption Being Challenged
D219 declared Louis's credentials "structurally incompatible with €2,000+/month closes" — meaning Louis cannot credibly sell at that price point regardless of who is on the call.

### Why the Assumption Is Incomplete

**Louis has tried zero price points.** The following have never been tested:
- €500/month for a single workflow
- €1,000/month for a basic managed service
- €2,000/month for a pilot engagement
- €7,500 for a pilot project

"Structurally incompatible" is a conclusion that requires evidence. The evidence required is: attempts to sell that failed due to Louis's credibility. There have been zero attempts.

The pulse process substituted a structural diagnosis for market evidence. This is the same error as "vertical commitment before network data" (challenged in the vertical audit) — deciding without data, then calling the decision structural.

### What This Means

The "incompatibility" claim cannot be validated without Louis making outreach attempts. The pulse process has declared failure before the experiment has run. Louis's credibility ceiling (D84) is an empirical claim that requires attempted sales to validate — not a structural constraint that excuses not trying.

## Challenge 5: No Commercial Path Has Been Validated by a Single Sales Attempt

### Assumption Being Challenged

### Why the Assumption Is Wrong

The following have never been tested:
- Any pricing tier at any price point
- Any outreach message to any prospect
- Any discovery call
- Any demo
- Any pilot conversion

**The commercial model exists entirely in hypothesis space.** Every decision — from D1 to D242 — was made without a single data point from actual market interaction. The pricing decisions (D6, D43, D72, D110, D149, D150, D174, D177, D179, D232) have been debated and reversed 10+ times without any of them ever being shown to a prospect.

This is not strategic refinement. This is spinning in hypothesis space.

### What This Means

The commercial model is not a strategy waiting for execution — it is a set of untested hypotheses. The correct sequence is: run one outreach attempt → get one "no" or "yes" → update the model based on real data. The pulse process has been running 45 cycles of model refinement without any cycle of market testing.
