3. Day 15: Based on real data, define Month 1 Alizé scope that fits within available hours

**Alizé's most viable Year 1 path (lean service business, per D172) is compatible with either label.**

## Agent 2: Pricing Contradiction Agent

**Contradiction challenged:** D166 vs D174 — direct conflict between Unit Economics (€5,000-7,500 pilot) and Pricing Architect (free pilot + proof period).

### Key Findings

**D166 wins on unit economics.** The math is concrete: €1,500 flat pilot doesn't cover 10-15 days of delivery. D174 ignores cost structure entirely.

**D174's free pilot creates worse problems.** Zero client commitment signal. "Charity case study" dynamic inverts power. Delivery credibility conflated with pricing credibility.

**D166's €5,000-7,500 is unsellable by Louis.** D84 explicitly states Louis's profile is a credibility ceiling at €7,500+ without a senior commercial co-founder. Unit economics are right but sales reality isn't there yet.

### Resolution: Staged Pilot with Proof Gates

| Stage | Timing | Price | Deliverable | Gate |

| Stage 1 Discovery Sprint | Week 1 | €500 (credited) | Scoped workflow, integration map, success metrics, signed scope | Client signs off on workflow before Stage 2 |

| Stage 4 Expansion | Month 4+ | €1,800-2,500/mo | Additional workflows, quarterly review | — |

No single payment exceeds Louis's credibility ceiling. Total pilot investment (€1,500) is structured to avoid charity model. Every stage requires payment and produces a proof gate simultaneously.

## Agent 3: GTM Contradiction Agent

**Contradiction challenged:** D172 vs D173 — "€1,500 productized project is primary revenue source" vs "warm referrals only after delivery."

### Key Findings

**The contradiction is a false one.** D172 describes the delivery/pricing model. D173 describes the second-order growth flywheel. They operate at different layers.

**D173 is about clients 2, 3, and 4.** D173 says warm referrals come from delivered clients — this is true. It does NOT say "do no outreach before delivery." The Auditor's cold email recommendation in Week 1 is precisely the mechanism that generates the first delivery event.

**"Productized fixed-fee project" is a delivery architecture, not a sales motion.** The €1,500 format makes cold outreach viable by removing negotiation ambiguity. Cold outreach → first delivery → warm referrals → more deliveries.

### Resolution: Month 1 GTM Sequence (Zero-Proof Starting Point)

1. **Week 1:** Buy alize.studio, deploy landing page with €1,500 offer, send 10-15 cold emails to ICP prospects
2. **Week 2:** Target 3-5 discovery calls booked from cold outreach; 20-min qualifying calls
3. **Week 3:** First client signs €1,500 fixed-fee scope; 50%/50% payment split
4. **Week 4:** Deliver first working agent; at handoff ask for one referral (first warm referral)

This sequence is internally consistent with both D172 and D173.
