## Argument 1: The Price Point Mismatch

### D84/D90/U86 assume €7,500+ deals. D125 sets pilot pricing at €1,500.

These are not minor revisions of the same conversation — they are categorically different sales situations:

| Price Point | Sales Complexity | Closer Profile Needed |
|-------------|-----------------|---------------------|
| €1,500 (D125) | Single call, scoped deliverable, professional services framing | Someone who can run a competent discovery call and close a €1,500 engagement |
| €7,500+ | Multi-stakeholder, negotiation, risk sharing, 30+ day cycles | Senior closer with PME relationships and credibility |

**At €1,500/2-week diagnostic:**
- The frame is "I hired a consultant for 2 weeks to assess my situation"
- Decision is often unilateral (DG can say yes without board approval)
- €1,500 is a consulting budget item, not a capital investment
- The pitch is essentially: "Give me €1,500 and 2 weeks, I'll give you clarity"

**At €7,500+:**
- The frame is "I'm signing a multi-thousand euro contract with an unknown startup"
- Requires peer references, social proof, trust architecture
- Multiple stakeholders often involved
- Risk of failure is politically significant for the buyer

Louis can plausibly close €1,500 consulting engagements. He plausibly cannot close €7,500+ engagements solo.

**The U86/D84/D90 framing should be re-tagged:** These decisions describe €7,500+ deal dynamics. They do not describe €1,500 diagnostic engagement dynamics. If D125 is the active pricing decision, U86 as stated is not the immediate blocker.

## Argument 2: The Timeline Mismatch

### Finding a senior commercial co-founder in 2 weeks is not realistic for a pre-revenue, unfunded startup.

D90 says "warm outreach blocked until closing-capable person exists." U93 asks "by what date is U86 resolved? Fallback if not secured by Month 1 Week 2?"

Let's be concrete about what "find a senior commercial co-founder" actually means:

**What a real commercial co-founder actually requires:**
1. Identification (1-2 weeks): Find candidates with French PME network, closing experience at €5k-20k deal sizes, willingness to join unfunded startup
2. Outreach/recruitment (2-4 weeks): Most senior people won't respond to cold outreach from unknown founders; requires warm introduction or significant persuasion
3. Due diligence (1-2 weeks): The person is joining as a co-founder — they'll vet Louis and Alizé as much as Louis vets them
4. Negotiation (1-2 weeks): Equity split, role definition, vesting — these conversations take time
5. Start date (2-4 weeks): If they're currently employed (which senior commercial people are), they typically need 2-3 months notice

**Realistic timeline: 3-6 months minimum**, assuming Louis has an existing warm network of senior sales/BD people.

**What U91 tells us:** Louis's actual committed hours to Alizé have been UNRESOLVED through 26 pulses. Until U91 is resolved, we don't even know if Louis has the bandwidth to run a co-founder search process.

**The D90 "outreach blocked until commercial layer exists" rule, combined with U86's 2-week resolution expectation, is internally contradictory.** You cannot simultaneously:
- Block outreach until a co-founder exists
- Find a co-founder in 2 weeks
- Be a pre-revenue unfunded startup

**The rule should be:** If the commercial co-founder is a prerequisite, the timeline must be realistic. If Month 1 outreach is important, the commercial layer definition must be achievable within Month 1.

## Argument 3: The Definition Problem

### "Closing-capable person" is never defined. The term obscures three very different profiles.

The debate log uses these phrases interchangeably:
- "Senior commercial co-founder" (U86)
- "Closing-capable person" (D90)
- "Senior commercial closer" (D84)

These describe fundamentally different roles with different availability profiles:

| Profile | What They Do | Availability | Cost | Realistic for Alizé? |
|---------|--------------|--------------|------|---------------------|
| **Sales Development Rep** | Qualify inbound, book calls, manage pipeline | Readily available, can hire Month 1 | €2-3k/month for junior | Yes — handles €1,500 closes |
| **Sales Coach/Script Reviewer** | Review Louis's outreach, roleplay calls, advise on positioning | One engagement, 2-4 calls | €500-1,500 flat | Yes — solves Louis's skills gap |
| **Senior Closer** | Runs the commercial call, negotiates, closes €7,500+ | Requires equity, co-founder-level recruitment | Equity + salary | No — not realistic in Month 1 |
| **Commercial Co-Founder** | Strategic partner, brings book of business, full GTM ownership | 3-6 month search minimum | Equity 20-40% | No — timeline incompatible with D90 |

**The critical question:** Which of these does U86 actually require?

If the answer is "someone who can close a €1,500 diagnostic engagement," then:
- A sales coach is sufficient
- Louis + a script review is sufficient
- A junior SDR with commission is sufficient

If the answer is "someone who can close €7,500+ engagements," then U86 correctly describes the blocker — but D125 makes that the wrong question for Month 1.

## Alternative Framing

### The Minimum Viable Commercial Layer for Month 1-2

**At €1,500/2-week diagnostic (D125), the commercial layer needed is:**

**Option A — Louis + Sales Coach (fastest, cheapest)**
- Louis runs the outreach and discovery calls
- A sales coach (2-4 sessions, €500-1,500) reviews his script and does 1-2 roleplay calls
- A peer or mentor with sales experience listens to his first 2-3 discovery calls as feedback
- **Timeline to operational:** 1 week
- **Cost:** €500-1,500
- **Who closes:** Louis, with preparation

**Option B — Louis + Junior SDR (Month 1-2 bridge)**
- Hire a junior SDR (or part-time freelance) at €1,500-2,000/month to handle outreach and pipeline management
- Louis closes the actual calls
- SDR manages the funnel, follow-ups, scheduling
- **Timeline to operational:** 2-3 weeks (hiring + onboarding)
- **Cost:** €1,500-2,000/month

**Option C — Senior Commercial Co-Founder (correct for €7,500+, wrong for D125)**
- Recruit a senior closer who can run the full commercial cycle
- Requires equity, 3-6 month timeline
- **This is the right answer for Phase 2 (when monthly retainers hit €3,000+), not Month 1**

### Pricing Threshold Framework

| Deal Size | Commercial Profile Needed | Realistic for Month 1? |
|-----------|-------------------------|------------------------|
| €0-500 | No commercial layer needed | Yes |
| €500-2,000 | Louis + script/coach | Yes |
| €2,000-5,000 | Louis + junior SDR + coach | Month 2+ |
| €5,000-10,000 | Senior closer or co-founder | Phase 2 (Month 4+) |
| €10,000+ | Commercial co-founder required | Phase 2+ |

**D125 pricing (€1,500) sits at the threshold where Louis + coach is sufficient.**

### The Real Blocker: Louis's Sales Skills Gap, Not a Missing Co-Founder

D84's diagnosis was incomplete. The issue is not "Louis lacks a co-founder." The issue is "Louis lacks sales experience."

These require different solutions:

| Root Cause | Right Solution | Timeline | Cost |
|------------|----------------|----------|------|
| Louis can't run a discovery call | Sales coach, roleplay, script review | 1-2 weeks | €500-1,500 |
| Louis has no PME network | U52 ICP mapping + warm outreach prep | 1 week | €0 |
| Louis can't close €7,500 deals | Senior closer or co-founder | 3-6 months | Equity |
| Louis lacks delivery skills | Practice delivery before client work (D122) | 4 weeks | €0 (internal) |

**U86 as stated conflates all four of these.** The right approach is to disaggregate them and solve the actual Month 1 bottleneck (sales skills) rather than treating the most extreme solution (commercial co-founder) as the prerequisite.
