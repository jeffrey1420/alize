# Alizé Research Pulse 10 — 2026-03-30 — Business Model Identity Debate

**Date:** 2026-03-30
**Pulse:** 10th research pulse
**Topic:** Is Alizé a SaaS business or a consulting practice wearing tech clothes?

---

## Assumption Challenged

> "Alizé is a managed AI agent service (SaaS-like recurring revenue)"

This framing is chosen because it sounds fundable and scalable. But it may not reflect economic reality for Years 1-2.

---

## The Honest Unit Economics

**Revenue at 6 clients (conservative warm-first GTM):**
- 6 clients × €1,200/month = €7,200/month = €86,400/year

**Costs at 6 clients:**
- Louis opportunity cost: €0 (he's an intern, but his time has real value if he had a job)
- Delivery partner (2 days/week): ~€3,000-4,000/month
- Infrastructure (OVHcloud, n8n, tools): ~€200-400/month
- Total: ~€3,200-4,400/month

**At 6 clients: Alizé generates €2,800-4,000/month net.** That's not a SaaS business. That's a part-time consulting practice with recurring clients.

**Revenue at 8 clients:**
- 8 × €1,200 = €9,600/month
- Net after costs: ~€5,200-6,400/month

**Revenue at 12 clients:**
- 12 × €1,200 = €14,400/month
- Net after costs (requires 2 delivery people): ~€8,000-10,000/month

**The reality:** Alizé behaves like a consulting practice until it hits 12+ clients. At 12 clients, it generates the revenue of one senior freelancer, not a VC-fundable SaaS business.

---

## The Consulting Practice Model

A consulting practice at 6-8 clients with:
- 1 senior delivery partner (€80-120K/year fully-loaded)
- 1 technical executor (Louis, equity/learning instead of full salary)
- 1 client-facing relationship owner (Louis or partner)

This works. It's a real business. But it's not a SaaS business and should not be pitched as one.

**The problem with the SaaS framing:**
- VCs want 80%+ gross margins, 3-5x ARR growth, and a path to €10M+ ARR
- A consulting practice at €7-14K/month with 40-60% net margins and 10-20% growth doesn't qualify
- If Louis raises a SaaS round with consulting practice metrics, he'll get a down round or no round
- If Louis raises a consulting/services round, he doesn't need VC

---

## The Network Exhaustion Risk Nobody is Counting

The warm-first GTM depends on Louis's network. But:

- Louis's direct network: 20-40 warm contacts (optimistic)
- Grinto's network: 10-30 warm contacts
- Combined: 30-70 warm contacts in Year 1
- Conversion rate: 10-15% = 3-10 pilots in Year 1 from network alone

After Year 1, the warm network is exhausted. Cold outbound at that point starts from zero with no brand, no case studies, and no credibility.

**The question is not "how do we get 6-8 clients?" The question is "what does Alizé look like after the network is gone?"**

---

## The Hybrid Possibility: "Productized Consulting"

The most honest framing is "productized consulting":

- Alizé sells repeatable processes (the pilot playbook, the monthly retainer structure)
- But each delivery is customized to the client
- The product is the *process*, not the *software*
- Scalability comes from: (a) raising prices, (b) hiring more delivery people, not (c) adding more software users

This is a legitimate and profitable business model. It's how the best French IT consulting firms operate. It's not as sexy as SaaS but it's more honest.

**The implication:** Alizé should raise prices to reflect what a premium consulting engagement costs in France: €800-1,200/day equivalent = €4,000-6,000/month per client. At that price point, 6 clients = €24-36K/month = real business.

---

## Verdict

Alizé is a productized consulting practice, not a SaaS business, for at least the first 24 months. This is not a weakness — it's a choice. It affects:

1. **GTM:** Sell consulting engagements, not software subscriptions
2. **Fundraising:** Bootstrap or raise a small pre-seed on consulting practice terms, not SaaS terms
3. **Hiring:** Hire delivery people, not engineers (until Month 12+)
4. **Pricing:** Raise prices to €2,000-3,000/month per client minimum — the market supports it for real business change

**3 Recommendations:**

1. **Reprice to reflect consulting reality:** At €1,200/month, Alizé is underpriced for the business change it delivers. €2,000-2,500/month per client is defensible for a serious operational change. At that price, 6 clients = €12-15K/month = real business.

2. **Accept the consulting identity publicly:** "Alizé deploys AI agents into your business — we manage everything, you get results." This framing sidesteps the "SaaS vs consulting" question and focuses on buyer outcome. It's also more accurate.

3. **Plan for network exhaustion now:** Year 1 should be spent building referral infrastructure and case studies that make cold outbound viable in Year 2. The warm network is a launchpad, not a long-term strategy.
