# Pulse25 Pilot Pricing Debate: Against "Free First Pilot" (D123)

**Author:** Alizé Pilot Pricing Agent  
**Date:** 2026-03-30  
**Decision in scope:** D123 — "Pilot 1 = free performance test (zero strings, internal use only). Pilot 2+ = full price with case study rights."

---

## Executive Summary

**D123 ("Free First Pilot") should be replaced.**  
Free signals weakness, attracts the wrong buyers, and poisons downstream pricing conversations. Full price is too aggressive for an unproven first engagement.  
**The answer is Option C: Time-Boxed Engagement at €1,500 for 2 weeks (€750/day), framed as "Diagnostic + Proof of Concept."**

---

## The Case Against D123 — Free First Pilot

### 1. Free Signals Desperation, Not Confidence

French PME/ETI decision-makers (DG, COO, DGA) are seasoned executives. They've seen dozens of vendors promise miracles and give away products to get foot in the door. When Alizé says "pilot 1 is free," the subtext they hear is: *"We don't trust our own results enough to charge you."*

A managed AI agent service positioning at €1,500–€2,500/month (plus €4,500–€7,500 setup) cannot afford to open with a zero-cost engagement. That pricing level requires Alizé to be perceived as a **trusted partner with proven delivery**, not a startup buying its way into an account.

### 2. Free Attracts Bargain Hunters, Not Premium Buyers

The wrong prospect profile:
- Takes the free pilot, uses it minimally, then demands a discount on ongoing pricing because "we already proved it works for us"
- Has no skin in the game → low engagement → poor pilot results → blames the vendor
- Shops the free proof-of-concept to 3 competitors and uses it as leverage in negotiations

The right prospect profile:
- Has a real operational problem they need solved
- Is willing to invest time and money to validate a solution properly
- Makes buying decisions based on value, not on who gives the most away for free

A paid engagement—even a small one—automatically filters for commitment.

### 3. "Internal Use Only" Kills the Case Study Pipeline

D123 explicitly limits Pilot 1 to "internal use only, zero strings." This means no public case study, no named reference, no external proof of value. But Alizé's go-to-market relies heavily on **social proof within the French PME/ETI ecosystem**—peer recommendations, sector references, demonstrable ROI numbers. With D123, every Pilot 1 client is a dead end for business development. Alizé would be giving away value with zero return on the investment.

### 4. Downstream Pricing Conversations Become Awkward

The transition from D123 to D123's own framework ("Pilot 2+ = full price with case study rights") creates a jarring moment:  
*"Thank you for your free pilot. Now for the full program at €4,500–€7,500 setup + €1,500–€2,500/month..."*  
The client will rightly ask: *"Why did you give it away free and now charge full price? What's different?"*

This is a trust-damaging conversation that Option C entirely avoids.

---

## Why Full Price (€4,500–€7,500 Pilot) Is Also Wrong

Jumping straight to full pilot pricing for an unproven relationship is aggressive for:
- **Prospect trust:** No established relationship, no demonstrated understanding of their business
- **Alizé's own risk:** If the prospect is not a good fit, Alizé has over-invested in scoping and discovery
- **Market positioning:** At €7,500+ for a first engagement with a PME (50–250 employees), there's real risk of sticker shock that kills the conversation before it starts

Full price without any relationship-building mechanism is as damaging as free.

---

## The Recommendation: Option C — Time-Boxed Engagement (€1,500 / 2 Weeks)

### The Structure
- **Duration:** 2 weeks (10 working days)
- **Price:** €1,500 flat (€750/day)
- **Framing:** "Diagnostic + Proof of Concept" — a professional services engagement, not a discount
- **Deliverables:** 
  1. Business process audit and AI integration opportunity map
  2. Technical proof-of-concept on 1–2 high-impact workflows
  3. Written findings + ROI estimate for full deployment
- **Downstream:** Clear path to full pilot at standard pricing, but no obligation

### Why Option C Beats D123 (Free Pilot)

| Dimension | D123 Free Pilot | Option C (€1,500 / 2 Weeks) |
|---|---|---|
| Signal to market | "We're unsure of our value" | "We know what we're doing and our time has value" |
| Client commitment | Zero — low stakes, low engagement | Skin in the game — higher engagement and follow-through |
| Filter for serious buyers | None — everyone takes free | Some commitment threshold — filters out casual browsers |
| Case study rights | "Internal use only" = no external proof | Can publish findings (anonymized) as diagnostic report |
| Downstream pricing | Awkward "now we charge" conversation | Natural progression: diagnostic → full pilot → ongoing |
| Alizé revenue per engagement | €0 | €1,500 (partial cost recovery) |

### Why Option C Beats Full Price (€7,500)

| Dimension | Full Price (€7,500) | Option C (€1,500 / 2 Weeks) |
|---|---|---|
| Prospect barrier | High — significant commitment before trust is built | Moderate — scoped, time-boxed, clearly defined |
| Risk for prospect | High outlay for unproven engagement | Contained, bounded risk |
| Framing | "Buy our product" | "Hire us to diagnose your problem and prove our approach" |
| Sales conversion | Lower — sticker shock for cold/early-stage prospect | Higher — accessible entry point with clear upgrade path |
| Professional positioning | Vendor selling a product | Consultant delivering insight |

### Why €750/Day Is the Right Day Rate

€750/day is a **credible French consulting day rate** for a senior practitioner in operational improvement or digital transformation. It's not entry-level (which would signal inexperience), and it's not partner-level (€1,500–€2,500/day), which would create an expectation of C-level involvement on every call.

At €750/day, Alizé is positioned as:
- **Competent and experienced** — not cheap labor
- **Accessible** — a PME can engage without board-level approval
- **Professional services** — not a SaaS product with a free trial

This framing is **deeply familiar to French DG/COO/DGA buyers**. They hire consultants, they understand day rates, and they respect engagements that have a clear beginning, middle, and end.

### The Alignment With Alizé's Business Model

Option C's 2-week diagnostic naturally transitions into:
1. **Full Pilot (€4,500–€7,500 setup)** — the natural next step after a successful diagnostic
2. **Ongoing managed service (€1,500–€2,500/month)** — with warm relationship and proven ROI already demonstrated

The pipeline is clean: **Diagnostic → Full Pilot → Retainer**. No pricing whiplash, no awkward "now we charge" conversation, no case study restrictions.

### What About U118 — Louis's Financial Capacity?

This concern actually **supports Option C over D123**:
- D123 gives Alizé €0 revenue while Louis still funds Month 1–3 operations — pure cash drain
- Option C gives Alizé €1,500/relevant engagement — not transformative, but partial cost recovery on delivery
- Every paid engagement builds Alizé's financial runway while the business is validated

## Why Not Option A (€500–750 Flat, "Scoping Workshop")?

Option A is the second-best choice, but it has weaknesses:
- **"Scoping workshop" framing** is softer and less credible than a 2-week diagnostic — it implies Alizé is scoping *for* a sale, not *delivering* value
- **€500–750 is cheap for a professional service** — this is the day rate of a junior consultant in France, not a premium managed service provider
- The framing doesn't hold up well if the prospect pushes back: "You charged me €600 for a workshop and now want €7,500 for a pilot?"

Option C's framing is harder to negotiate down and more defensible.

---

## Why Not Option B (Success-Based, 15% of Savings)?

Option B has theoretical elegance (aligned incentives!) but fatal practical problems in the French PME market:
- **Measurement complexity:** "Time savings" is notoriously hard to attribute — which system saved time? The AI agent or the process redesign? The DG will argue endlessly
- **Administrative burden:** Tracking and verifying savings for 6 months requires ongoing client involvement and reporting infrastructure
- **Cultural mismatch:** French business culture is contract-focused and litigious — a "15% of savings" model will require ironclad, complex contracts that cost more to draft than they're worth
- **Revenue unpredictability:** Alizé cannot model runway or hiring on "maybe 15% of whatever we save them"

Option B is a VC-style Silicon Valley model. It doesn't translate to Lyon or Paris PME buyers.

---

## Why Not Option D (€7,500 with Money-Back Guarantee)?

Option D has the right confidence signal but the wrong risk/reward:
- **Money-back guarantee** requires Alizé to define "success metrics" upfront — which is nearly impossible for a first engagement where both parties are still learning the client's processes
- **€7,500 upfront + refund risk** is a worse financial outcome than €1,500 flat if the deal falls through
- The guarantee adds legal complexity and sets up a adversarial dynamic ("you owe me my money back because X didn't happen")
- **Sticker shock** for a first cold or warm engagement with a 50–250 person company is real

Option D is appropriate for a **repeat client or a partner-level engagement**, not for the first entry point.

**Final recommendation: Replace D123 with Option C — Time-Boxed Engagement at €1,500 for 2 weeks, framed as "Diagnostic + Proof of Concept."**

This is the right balance: not free (preserves value perception), not full price (accessible enough to start the relationship), professionally framed (trusted by French DG/COO/DGA), and naturally leads into the full pilot → retainer pipeline.

## Appendix: Sample Prospect Conversation

> **Alizé:** "We don't do free pilots. What we do is a 2-week diagnostic engagement — €1,500, flat fee. We spend 10 days inside your operations, identify the 2–3 highest-impact workflows where AI agents would deliver ROI, build a technical proof of concept on at least one, and deliver a written report with findings and a roadmap for full deployment. It's a professional services engagement. If the work delivers value and you want to continue, the path to our full pilot program is clear. If not, you have our findings and owe us nothing further."

This is a completely different conversation than:  
> "We'll give you a free performance test, internal use only, no strings — and then we'll talk about pricing."
