## CHALLENGE TO EXISTING DECISIONS

### Challenge 1: D82's "3-6 Month Window" Is Mislabeled

D82 states the effective window is 3-6 months. This framing assumes the clock starts NOW. It does not. The 3-6 month window begins when:

1. Louis has quantified, committed, and ring-fenced hours
2. A closing-capable person exists (U86)
3. Grinto conflict is contractually resolved (U87)
4. Warm network ICP is mapped (U88)

**Until those four things are true, no effective window exists — only prerequisite resolution time.**

If each of these takes 1-3 months (reasonable given U86 and U87 are contractual/recruiting timelines), the effective window doesn't start until Month 3-4 at earliest. The "3-6 month window" is therefore more accurately described as **"2-3 months of execution preceded by 3-4 months of setup."**

### Challenge 2: D90-D93 May Be Over-Rigid

D90 states warm outreach is blocked until a closing-capable person exists. D93 states U86 and U89 are prerequisites. But is this binary framing correct?

**Counter-argument:** Warm outreach could proceed with:
- Louder personal credibility (Louis as known operator, not Alizé rep)
- Narrower ICP (only warmest 10-15 accounts where Louis has direct relationships)
- Explicit framing as "initial conversations" not "sales cycle"
- Co-founder recruited in parallel, not before

The question is whether the cost of waiting (D87) exceeds the cost of running a limited warm-up with ring-fenced guardrails.

### Challenge 3: D87 (Grinto Conflict) May Not Be a Full Stop

The Grinto conflict may be manageable via explicit ring-fencing rather than requiring full resolution:
- Louis does not mention Alizé to Grinto clients
- Louis does not use Grinto IP, data, or relationships
- Louis does not position Alizé as competitive to Grinto
- Legal review confirms no contractual prohibition on side ventures

If Grinto's contract has no non-compete and only basic confidentiality, the conflict is manageable, not blocking.

### Challenge 4: D91's "25+ Hours/Week" Is Arbitrary Without U91

D91 sets 25 hours/week as a threshold without knowing if Louis can actually deliver that. This is planning based on aspiration, not data. If Louis can only commit 15 hours, the plan needs to adjust, not pretend 25 is required.
