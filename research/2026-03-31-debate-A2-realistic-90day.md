## Constraint Analysis

**What this time budget actually allows per week:**
- 5 hrs = 300 minutes
- Outreach (research + message + follow-up): 2-3 hrs

- Technical work: 1-2 hrs
- Admin, prep, thinking: 30-60 min

**What this time budget cannot allow:**
- Content marketing (LinkedIn posts, articles, SEO)
- Partner channel development
- Event attendance
- Building a "portfolio" of agents or case studies
- ANY parallel development track (Kuroba AND Alizé with equal weight)
- The pulse process itself (67 pulses of research = displacement activity, per P69)

**The structural mismatch (P69-D7):**
Alizé's GTM requires **outbound sales** — prospecting, discovery calls, proposal conversations, relationship management. Louis's demonstrated profile is **technical execution** (building Kuroba). These are not the same skill set. More critically: they compete for the same hours. At 5-8/week, you cannot be both a salesperson and a technical builder in the same week.

## The Math That Doesn't Work

### Assumption 1: "Month 1: buy domain + begin warm outreach"

**This is technically achievable but represents only half the challenge.**

The real question isn't "can Louis send 5 emails in Month 1?" It's "can Louis do outreach AND technical setup AND discovery calls within 5-8 hrs/week?"

Answer: Barely, and not well. Each discovery call consumes an entire week's outreach capacity. If Month 1 includes one discovery call, Louis has ~4 hrs left for everything else.

**The math:**
- Warm outreach to 5 contacts: 3-4 hrs (research, personalize, send, follow-up)
- Discovery call preparation + call: 2 hrs
- Remaining for technical setup, landing page, domain: 1-2 hrs
- **Result:** Rushed everything.

### Assumption 2: "First pilot in Month 1-2"

**This is achievable ONLY if pilots are pre-templated and narrow-scoped.**

The GTM plan implies each pilot requires discovery + scoping + design + deployment + training. That's 15-20 hours at minimum. At 5-8 hrs/week:
- 15 hours = 2-3 weeks of full-time work
- Spread across 4-8 weeks = barely any progress each week

**The real constraint:** Pilot delivery time must compress to ≤8 hours of technical work, or Month 2 produces nothing billable.

**Critical dependency not in the GTM plan:** Who delivers the pilot? If Louis is doing outreach AND pilot delivery, he cannot do both in the same week. One discovery call = zero technical work that week.

### Assumption 3: "Year 1 target: 6-8 clients"

**Let's do the actual math.**

Assume:
- 5 hrs/week × 52 weeks = 260 hrs/year
- Outreach: ~2 hrs/week = 104 hrs/year
- Discovery + sales: ~1.5 hrs/week = 78 hrs/year
- Technical delivery + management: ~1.5 hrs/week = 78 hrs/year

**Contact volume possible:**
- 2 hrs/week × 50 weeks = 100 hrs for outreach
- 5 contacts researched + messaged per hr = 500 contact attempts
- At 10% response rate = 50 conversations
- At 20% discovery call booking = 10 discovery calls
- At 30% pilot conversion = 3 pilots
- At 66% pilot-to-recurring = 2 recurring clients

**Year 1 realistic outcome: 1-2 paying clients, €500-1,600/month MRR.**

The 6-8 client target requires either:
- 3x the weekly hours (15-20 hrs/week), OR
- A channel that produces inbound leads without outbound effort (SEO takes 12-18 months; events require presence)

The Year 1 target is not "ambitious" — it's mathematically disconnected from the stated time investment.

## Recommendation: MODIFY THE PLAN — Significantly

### VERDICT: MODIFY, not HOLD, not KILL

Alizé is viable as a **very small, very slow side business** — not as a startup as currently designed. The plan needs a complete re-scope, not a deferral.

### What "MODIFY" Means — Specific Changes

**1. Year 1 target: 6-8 clients → 1-2 clients**
A 6-8 client Year 1 requires dedicated sales capacity Louis doesn't have. One to two clients at €500-1,000/month = €6,000-12,000/year. This is a profitable side project, not a venture business. Adjust ambition accordingly or don't start.

**2. Month 1-2 first pilot → Month 3-4 first pilot**
The pilot timeline in the GTM plan assumes 15-20 hrs/week for pilot delivery. At 5-8 hrs/week, the pilot takes 2-3x longer. Push Month 1-2 pilot to Month 3-4. Use Month 1-2 to send messages and find the right prospect.

**3. Outbound-only → Outbound-only with ruthlessly narrow focus**
Alizé's GTM plan includes LinkedIn content, SEO, events, and partner channels. None of these are executable at 5-8 hrs/week alongside outbound and delivery. Kill all channels except warm outreach to personal network until monthly hours increase.

**4. Parallel Kuroba track → Sequential (if at all)**
P69's findings are clear: Kuroba and Alizé compete for the same hours. If Louis spends 3 hrs/week on Kuroba and 3 hrs/week on Alizé, he has 1-2 hrs/week left for everything else. Neither makes progress. Choose one.

**5. Any mention of "AI employee" framing → REMOVE (per Pulse 67)**
French labor law + political narrative makes "employee" framing a liability. Replace with "workflow automation" or "opérateur IA."

**6. Pilot delivery: custom every time → templated agent + narrow scope**
The only way pilot delivery works at 5-8 hrs/week is if the agent template is pre-built, the scope is ruthlessly narrow (one workflow, one integration, no RAG in Month 1), and onboarding is scripted.

### The Uncomfortable Question (from P69-D9)

**What would make Louis send one email?**

Not more research. Not another pulse. Not another decision log.

The answer is structural: the current setup provides the subjective experience of progress (research, decisions, plans) without requiring the discomfort of external action (sending messages to humans who may not reply).

A realistic 90-day plan that Louis will actually execute requires:
1. **Fewer decisions, more actions** — reduce the plan to 3-4 steps, not 15
2. **Accountability partner or hard deadline** — external commitment that produces consequences for not sending
3. **Sequencing, not simultaneity** — one track at a time (outreach → discovery → pilot), not three tracks in parallel
4. **Permission to fail quietly** — if Alizé produces 1 client in Year 1, that's not failure at 5-8 hrs/week. It's success for a side project.
