# Pulse 13 — The Website Debate: First Blocker or False Constraint?

**Date:** 2026-03-30
**Role:** GTM Strategist (website dependency analysis)
**Context:** D47 flags "live website at alize.studio" as the first cold outbound blocker. Domain not purchased. Louis has warm network contacts. Brief says Phase 1 = domain + hosting + landing page.

---

## The Question

Is a live website genuinely required before Alizé can start commercial activity? Or is the "website first" framing an artifact of default SaaS GTM thinking that doesn't apply to a productized consulting practice selling €4,500 pilots via warm networks?

---

## Challenged Assumption #1

**Assumption:** The website is the first blocker because it enables cold outbound.

**Why it may be wrong:** This conflates two separate GTM channels that should run on different timelines. D26 already resolved that network-first warm outreach is the **primary** Year 1 channel, with cold outbound as a secondary play beginning Month 2+. But D47 treats "live website" as a universal first blocker — applying the cold outbound requirement to the warm outreach motion that doesn't need it.

When Louis reaches into his warm network (Grinto contacts, personal connections), those recipients don't need a website to decide whether to take a meeting. They're deciding based on: (a) who Louis is, (b) whether he understands their business, (c) whether the offer is relevant. None of those require alize.studio to be live.

**What to do differently:** Separate the timeline. Warm outreach to Louis's network can begin immediately using LinkedIn + direct messages. Cold outbound (which does need credibility infrastructure) follows Month 2+ after the website, case study, and ICP are locked. Don't let the slower track gatekeep the faster track.

---

## Challenged Assumption #2

**Assumption:** A website is the minimum viable commercial presence for a €4,500 consulting offer.

**Why it may be wrong:** For a SaaS product, the website is the product. For a productized consulting service selling to warm-network prospects, the minimum viable commercial presence is Louis himself — his LinkedIn profile, his reputation, his ability to communicate clearly, and the internal materials he can share in a private conversation. The market validation research (Section 5) confirms this: in 73% of French PME AI purchases, the DG drives the decision and "proximité" (personal trust) is critical. French SME leaders buy from people they know and trust, not from websites.

A landing page is a cold outbound asset. It's nearly irrelevant for warm-network deals where Louis can send a personalized Loom video, a shared Google Doc proposal, or even a direct calendar link — all of which require zero website infrastructure.

**What to do differently:** Redefine "commercial presence" for this channel. The MVP for warm outreach is: (1) Louis's LinkedIn profile positioned around AI agent deployment, (2) a clear verbal and written pitch, (3) a one-page scope document for the €4,500 pilot, and (4) optionally a shared Notion/Google Doc page that can be sent to a warm prospect. None of these require a domain purchase or hosting setup. Louis could literally send a Loom video tomorrow and start scheduling discovery calls.

---

## What Louis Actually Needs Before His First Pilot

The real dependencies for closing a warm-network pilot are different from what's currently sequenced:

| Requirement | Website needed? |
|-------------|-----------------|
| Clear pilot scope document (€4,500, deliverables, exit criteria) | No |
| Delivery capability (Louis + Gabin or contractor) | No |
| Discovery call with warm prospect | No |
| Internal pitch materials (Loom, Google Doc, Notion) | No |
| Case studies / social proof | No (first pilot can be "first of its kind") |
| Cold outbound credibility infrastructure | **Yes** |

---

## New Decision Required

**Decision D51: Parallelize warm outreach and website build — don't sequence them.**

**Recommendation:** Louis should begin warm outreach to his network **this week** using LinkedIn + direct materials, in parallel with the website build. The website is still built — it enables cold outbound, partner channels, and future inbound. But it no longer gates the first pilot.

**Unresolved question:** Does Louis have a sufficient body of internal materials ready to send in a warm outreach context (pitch deck, scope template, pilot one-pager)? If not, that's the real first blocker — not the website. Someone needs to produce those warm outreach materials urgently, before the website is ready, so the parallel track is actually viable.

---

## Summary

The "website as first blocker" framing is a cold outbound requirement masquerading as a universal prerequisite. For warm-network-led sales — which D26 identifies as Alizé's primary Year 1 channel — the website is not a blocker. It's a future-state nice-to-have. Louis's network contacts don't need alize.studio to decide whether to take a meeting. They need Louis to reach out, articulate the value, and propose a relevant pilot.

**The real first blocker is not the website. It's the absence of warm outreach materials and the absence of outreach itself.**
