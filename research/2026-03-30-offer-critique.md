## 3. Why the Pricing Loop is Unproductive

**The pricing research is avoidance dressed as diligence.**

Louis has run 30+ pulses. He hasn't bought alize.studio. He hasn't tracked a single hour. He hasn't emailed anyone. Every pulse that asks "what should I charge?" is a pulse not spent asking "will anyone actually pay me?"

Pricing research without customer contact is a feedback loop with no signal. He doesn't know if €1,500 is too high because he hasn't asked anyone. He doesn't know if €7,500 is viable because no one has told him.

**Break the loop:**
1. Pick ANY number (€1,500 diagnostic is fine)
2. Go talk to 10 people this week
3. If they say it's too expensive, lower it
4. If they say yes, raise it

The pricing will calibrate itself against real market resistance. Research can't do that.
