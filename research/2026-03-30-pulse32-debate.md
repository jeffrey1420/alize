## Agent 1: Pricing-Brief Auditor

**Task:** Compare BRIEF.md pricing against the unit economics findings from Pulse 31.

**Brief.md Pricing (as written):**
- Diagnostic: €490 (credited toward deployment)
- Pilot setup: €3,000-6,000
- Managed service: €800-1,500/month per agent

**Unit Economics Findings (Pulse 31):**
- Delivery cost: €400-450/day × 10-15 days = €4,000-6,750 per pilot
- Louis time: €200/day × 3 days pilot = €600
- Minimum viable monthly price (cost-plus): €7,963/month
- Current €800-1,500/month retainer = 10-19% of cost-plus minimum

**Assumption challenged:** "BRIEF.md pricing is internally consistent"

**Verdict: The assumption is false. BRIEF.md pricing is structurally below cost-plus across all tiers.**

### Key Conflicts

**Conflict 1: Pilot fee vs. delivery cost**
- BRIEF.md: €3,000-6,000 pilot setup
- Unit economics: €4,000-6,750 delivery cost alone
- At €3,000 pilot fee with €6,750 delivery cost: Alizé loses €3,750 per pilot
- The "€3,000" floor is below the cost floor for any realistic delivery

**Conflict 2: Monthly retainer vs. cost-plus minimum**
- BRIEF.md: €800-1,500/month per agent
- Cost-plus minimum: €7,963/month
- At €1,000/month: Alizé would need 8 clients to cover Louis's time alone (€4,000/month) before any delivery partner cost

**Conflict 3: €490 diagnostic vs. €750/day reality**
- BRIEF.md: €490 diagnostic (credited toward deployment)
- Market rate for qualified consultant: €600-900/day
- €490 is 54-82% below day rate for a 1-day diagnostic deliverable

**What needs to change in BRIEF.md:**
1. Pilot setup: €3,000-6,000 → €5,000-8,000 minimum
2. Monthly retainer: €800-1,500 → €3,500-5,000/month or acknowledge this is a loss-leader for a different model
3. Diagnostic: €490 → €1,500-2,000 (full day rate) or remove the credit mechanism entirely
4. Add explicit note that early pilots may be loss-leader CAC investments with explicit payback period
