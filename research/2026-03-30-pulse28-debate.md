## Debate 1: WTP vs Copilot — "Additive" Framing Fails Price Math

**Challenged:** D129 — "Position Alizé as additive to Copilot, not competitive"

**Verdict:** The additive framing is mathematically hostile to deal closure.

**Key arguments:**
- "Additive" forces buyers to stack budgets. For a 50-person PME: M365 (€1,105/mo) + Copilot (€1,500/mo) + Alizé (€1,500-2,500/mo) = €4,105-5,105/mo total AI stack. No French PME CFO approves this.
- Copilot anchors price perception at €30/user. Alizé at €1,500-2,500/month flat reads as 50-80x per-user cost vs Copilot — without the framing resetting the anchor.
- "Additive" implicitly concedes Copilot is sufficient. The buyer asks: "then why pay more?"
- French SME buyers (owner/COO) don't think in AI stacks. They want ONE vendor who solves ONE problem.

**Recommendation:** D129 revised to "the agent layer for companies where Copilot isn't enough."
- Old: "We work alongside Copilot to extend what's possible"
- New: "Copilot helps your team type faster. Alizé eliminates the work your team shouldn't be doing."
- New: "If Copilot was enough, you wouldn't need us. Most companies find it's not."

**What changes if additive framing fails:**
- Sales playbook shifts to treating "we have Copilot" as a buying signal (buyer is frustrated, not satisfied)
- Pricing must anchor on "replaces manual work, not adds to Microsoft"
- ICP shifts to: Copilot rejectors, low-adoption users, greenfield non-M365 buyers

## Debate 3: Category Framing — "Service Firm" Is Not a Category

**Challenged:** D133 — "French AI service firm for regulated industries"

**Verdict:** It's a vendor description, not a buyer category. It describes what Alizé is, not what the buyer gets.

**Key arguments:**
- "AI service firm" → French buyers hear SSII/ESN (body-shopping consultancies). Stigma: expensive, inconsistent, scope creep.
- "Regulated industries" → signals expertise but also complexity and long sales cycles.
- All 5 previous framings failed because they're inside-out (what Alizé is) rather than outside-in (what the buyer gets).
- French SME buyers think: "I need to automate these 47 manual reporting tasks." Never: "I need an AI service firm."

**Recommended category:** "AI-powered workflow automation for regulated SMEs"
- Lead with problem/solution: "Stop doing compliance work manually — AI does it for you"
- Website headline: "AI workflow automation for regulated SMEs — deploy in 4 weeks"
- Outreach subject lines: "Automate your compliance reporting: 60% less manual work"
- Demo structure: before/after workflow for a specific task, not platform tour
