## Evidence-Positioning Loop Audit

### The Loop

D227 states: "Stop positioning debates until one pilot with named client and specific metrics exists."

The problem this creates is a precise logical trap:

1. **Positioning requires pilot evidence to be "real"** (D227's premise)
2. **Pilots cannot be sold without coherent positioning** (D226 itself implies this — the hero sentence only works if you can say it to a prospect)
3. **Therefore, you cannot get pilots without positioning, and you cannot refine positioning without pilots**
4. **Result: complete commercial paralysis, justified as rigor**

This is not a methodological safeguard. It is a circular dependency that has been dressed up as a sensible pause. The 43 prior positioning debates did not fail because evidence was missing. They failed because Louis did not act on them. D227 converts Louis's inaction into a structural justification for more inaction.

---

### Assumption Smuggled by D227: Precision vs. Direction

**The assumption:** That positioning work is *binary* — either it is "proven" (has pilot evidence) or it is "speculative" (no evidence) and therefore worthless for commercial use.

**This is false.** Positioning work exists on a spectrum:

| Level | What It Is | Requires Pilot Evidence? |
|-------|-----------|--------------------------|
| **Claim** | "We save companies 10 hours/week on invoice processing" | YES — requires real client measurement |
| **Hypothesis** | "French PME lose significant time on repetitive admin tasks" | NO — supported by market research, labor economics |
| **Category** | "We serve 50-200 person service companies with back-office automation" | NO — can be derived from ICP definition |
| **Differentiator** | "We deploy and manage; you don't configure anything" | NO — can be stated from product definition |
| **Credibility signal** | "Here is a named client with verified results" | YES — requires pilot completion |

D227 conflates all five levels. It treats "positioning" as if it means level 1 (specific quantified claims) when most of the prior 43 debates were about levels 2-4 (category, problem, differentiator). You cannot get level 5 without selling. But you can absolutely act on levels 2-4 right now.

**The specific error in D227:** It uses the hardest level of positioning evidence (level 1 and 5: named client + specific metrics) to justify pausing ALL positioning work, including work that requires no evidence at all (levels 2-4). This is a bait-and-switch. "You need a named client to prove ROI" is true. "Therefore stop all positioning work including your ICP definition" is not logical.

---

### Assumption Smuggled by D227: Selling vs. Proving

**The assumption:** That positioning must be *proven* before it can be *used* in sales conversations.

**This inverts the function of sales.** Sales conversations are not showcases for proven positioning. They are the mechanism by which positioning gets tested and refined. Every "no" or "I don't understand" or "why would I pay that" is positioning feedback. D227's logic would have you wait until you have a perfect product to show anyone — but you cannot know what the perfect product is without showing it to anyone.

The actual function of a sales conversation with imperfect positioning:
- Surface where the prospect's mental model conflicts with your framing
- Identify which pain point lands and which doesn't
- Reveal whether your price anchor is reasonable for the prospect's budget
- Test whether your category label makes sense to the buyer

None of this requires pilot evidence. It requires a conversation.

D227 assumes pilots are the input to positioning. The truth is the reverse: positioning conversations are the input to pilots. You qualify the pilot fit through positioning. You don't close the positioning first and then discover you have a pilot.

## The Middle Path

### What Positioning CAN Be Fixed Before Pilot Evidence

**1. ICP definition (level 2-3, no evidence required)**
The ICP ("50-200 person service companies, 3+ business tools, repetitive admin tasks, prefer French providers") is already defined in BRIEF.md. This does not require a single pilot. It is a targeting decision based on market structure, not on Alizé's internal proof. D226's hero sentence already targets this. No evidence required.

**2. Category label (no evidence required)**
"Managed AI agent service" vs. "AI automation platform" vs. "AI employee service" can be debated and decided without any client evidence. These are framing choices based on competitive analysis and buyer psychology research — both already done in the prior pulses.

**3. Differentiator from Agentova (no evidence required)**
"Agentova is self-service; Alizé is managed" is already established. This differentiator requires no client evidence — it is a product model distinction that can be verified by visiting Agentova's website.

**4. Problem statement framing (no evidence required)**
"Repetitive back-office data tasks that senior staff waste time on" is a problem framing that requires no pilot. It is validated by basic labor economics and the ICP's own employee experience.

**5. Proof of concept capability (no named client required)**
Deploying a working agent for a self-selected internal workflow (even Kuroba) demonstrates the capability exists. This is not "pilot evidence" but it is capability evidence. The D226 sentence can be matched to this capability without a named external client.

### What Specifically Requires Pilot Evidence

**1. Quantified ROI claims** — "10 hours/week saved" requires a real measurement from a real client. Cannot be claimed without it.

**2. Reference client quotes** — Cannot exist without a client who agreed to be named.

**3. Case study metrics** — Before/after comparisons require a completed engagement.

### The Middle Path Between 43 Debates and Zero

The middle path is: **adopt D226 as the working positioning, run 3-5 discovery calls, refine from feedback.**

This is neither "43 more positioning debates" nor "wait for a named client." It is:
1. Take D226's hero sentence as the current best hypothesis
2. Book discovery calls using that framing
3. Listen for where it lands and where it doesn't
4. Update after 5 calls, not after 43 debates

The discovery call IS the positioning refinement mechanism. D227 treats positioning as something you get right before you sell. The middle path treats positioning as something you test by selling.

---

## Recommendation

D227 should be replaced with a narrower, more honest claim: "Stop making quantified ROI claims until one pilot with named client and specific metrics exists." This is true and defensible. What D227 gets wrong is the scope — it halts ALL positioning work when only the specific claims (level 1 and 5) actually require pilot evidence. The rest of the positioning stack (ICP, category, differentiator, problem framing) can and should be used immediately to drive discovery calls, which then generate the evidence D227 correctly identifies as missing. The chicken-and-egg loop dissolves once you stop requiring proof before conversation and start treating sales as the mechanism that produces proof.
