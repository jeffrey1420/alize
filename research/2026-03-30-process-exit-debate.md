# Alizé Process Exit Debate — 2026-03-30

**Role:** Process Critic  
**Context:** Pulse 31 spawned despite Pulse 30's own critic saying "process is documenting indecision." U91 and U52 unresolved for 30+ consecutive pulses. 160+ decisions catalogued. Zero external outreach executed.

---

## The Core Question

Is the research pulse process helping Alizé launch, or is it generating documents that simulate progress while the actual business remains stationary?

**Answer: It is hurting.**

---

## Assumption Being Challenged

> "Continuous multi-agent debate improves Alizé's positioning"

**This assumption is false — or at minimum, wildly overstated.**

Multi-agent debate has a valid use case: surfacing blind spots in plans that have already been made. It does NOT generate the foundational data that Alizé's plans are missing. The debate structure cannot answer:

- How many hours Louis actually works (U91) — requires a time-tracking app, not an agent
- How many of Louis's warm contacts fit the ICP (U52) — requires LinkedIn and a spreadsheet, not a debate
- What an expert-comptable actually does day-to-day — requires a conversation with one, not an argument about their workflows

More critically: the debate process has a **negative output rate** on decisions. After 30 pulses:

- D126 superseded D44 (vertical changed twice in 5 pulses)
- D149 superseded D110 (pilot pricing changed in the same pulse session that resolved it)
- D125 superseded D123 (free pilot killed within one pulse)
- D155/D156 killed D148/D149 within Pulse 30

When a process produces more decisions that kill previous decisions than it produces decisions that hold, it is not improving positioning. It is **churning.** Each superseded decision required someone's time to produce, review, and log. That time is gone.

The assumption that debate improves positioning treats discussion as a proxy for progress. It is not. Progress is: Louis talks to a prospect, gets a yes or no, learns from the answer, adjusts. Zero pulses have produced a yes or no from a prospect. 30 pulses have produced documents.

---

## The 3 Most Critical Flaws

### Flaw 1: The Pulse Process Cannot Resolve Data Gaps — and Keeps Pretending It Can

U91 (Louis's hours) and U52 (Louis's network ICP map) are listed as unresolved in Pulse 1. They are unresolved in Pulse 31. Every pulse between has produced new decisions that depend on these gates being resolved — and then been surprised when the gates remain unresolved.

The pattern:
1. Pulse debates pricing/GTM/vertical
2. Agent notes that decision depends on U91/U52
3. Agent recommends resolving U91/U52 first
4. Pulse concludes with new decisions that assume values for U91/U52
5. Next pulse identifies contradiction because U91/U52 still unresolved
6. Cycle repeats

U91 and U52 are Louis's personal data points. They cannot be synthesized. They cannot be debated into existence. They require Louis to spend 2-4 hours with his calendar and LinkedIn. No agent will ever produce them.

**The harm:** Every pulse that produces a GTM or pricing decision before U91/U52 resolves is generating noise. D145 (expert-comptable vertical conditional on network map), D149 (warm network pricing), D91 (25+ hours commitment gate) — all of these are conditionally valid and unconditionally premature. Treating them as settled decisions poisons the planning foundation.

### Flaw 2: The Process Incentivizes New Decisions Over Executed Decisions

The TODO.md resolved section has grown to ~100 decisions across 30 pulses. The unresolved section has grown proportionally. Every pulse adds items to both columns. Nothing has been executed.

This is not a backlog problem. This is a **validation problem.** The process has no mechanism for:
- Taking a decision out of debate because external reality has answered it
- Closing a U-item because Louis did the work
- Stopping a D-item from being relitigated because a future pulse "challenged" it

The closest thing to execution: Pulse 30 noted "Do NOT start cold outbound until website is live." The website is not live. The domain was not bought. No one has checked. But the pulse log notes it as a decision, which creates the appearance of progress.

**The harm:** The decision log has become a graveyard. Decisions arrive, get debated, get superseded, get buried. The graveyard grows. The business does not. Louis reviewing the TODO.md sees 100+ decisions and feels informed. He is not informed — he has a longer list of things that were considered and discarded.

### Flaw 3: Zero External Validation in 30+ Pulses

After 30+ research pulses, Alizé has:
- Zero discovery calls completed
- Zero pilots scoped
- Zero outreach messages sent
- Zero prospect conversations

The process has produced an extraordinary volume of internal documents about what prospects *might* want, what *might* resonate, what *might* close. None of it has been tested against an actual human who is not part of the research team.

Pulse 22 identified that "Louis needs a sales coach before outreach." Pulse 28 noted "warm outreach begins Month 1." Neither happened. The process keeps planning the first step without ever taking it, then plans around it in the next pulse.

**The harm:** Every decision in the TODO.md is a hypothesis. A hypothesis untested for 30 pulses is not a "considered decision" — it is an assumption that has been argued about extensively without being validated. The French PME market does not care how many pulses produced Alizé's positioning. It will respond or not respond based on what Louis says when he finally calls.

## Concrete EXIT CONDITION

The pulses should stop when **any one** of the following is true:

**Option A — Data gates cleared:**
- U91 resolved (Louis has tracked 2 weeks of actual hours and made the side-project/startup binary choice)
- U52 resolved (Louis has mapped 50+ warm contacts against ICP and counted ICP-fit contacts)

**Option B — External validation milestone:**
- Louis has completed 5 discovery calls with actual prospects (not roleplay, not internal)
- At least 1 prospect has said yes or no to a diagnostic offer

**Option C — Explicit deferral:**
- Louis explicitly declares Alizé a side-project for the foreseeable future
- Louis explicitly delegates all commercial activity to a named person
- No further pulse activity is warranted under side-project constraints

**The current state (U91 unresolved, U52 unresolved, zero outreach, no named commercial delegate) fails all three exit conditions.**

If pulses continue past this point without clearing these conditions, they are not research. They are activity that resembles research while being the opposite.

## What Should Replace the Pulse Process (Next 2 Weeks)

### Week 1: Louis Does His Homework

**Monday-Tuesday:**
- Track actual hours across Grinto, M1, web agency, personal for 7 days (U91)
- Export LinkedIn contacts, map first 50-75 against ICP: company size, role, operational admin pain, decision-maker access (U52)

**Wednesday:**

- Based on U91 data: make the binary choice — side-project or startup commitment. Write it down. Date it.

**Thursday-Friday:**
- Find a sales coach: €500-1,500 for 2-4 sessions. Someone with PME closing experience. Not a co-founder search — a coach who can review Louis's outreach materials and do 2-3 roleplay calls.
- Draft 3 outreach messages. Get them reviewed by the coach. Revise.
- Identify the 5 warmest, most ICP-fit prospects. Not "best potential clients" — the ones Louis could actually get a meeting with next week.

**Weekend:**
- Send first outreach. Get a response or don't. Learn from the response or the silence.

### Week 2: First External Contact

**Goal: 5 discovery calls booked or explicitly rejected by ICP-fit prospects.**

If Louis cannot book 5 calls in week 2 with ICP-fit prospects using his warm network, the warm-network-as-primary-channel thesis (D26, D59) is falsified and Alizé needs a different GTM plan.

The output of week 2 is not a document. It is:
- A calendar with 5 meetings scheduled, OR
- A written record of who said no and why, which is actual market data

### What Happens to the Pulse Process

The pulse process should be **suspended for 2 weeks.** Not cancelled — suspended. If Louis completes U91/U52, talks to 5 real prospects, and gets real feedback, the pulse process can resume with actual market data as input rather than hypothetical debates.

If Louis does not do the homework — if Week 1 passes and U91/U52 are still unresolved — the pulse process has been revealed as a procrastination mechanism. It should be cancelled, not suspended.

## The Bottom Line

The pulse process is not helping Alizé launch. It is providing a structure that feels like work while deferring the only work that matters: Louis talking to a prospect.

The decisions it has produced are extensive and internally contradictory. The 160 decisions in the log include multiple sets of directly conflicting choices (vertical: professional services vs expert-comptables vs confirmed-network; pricing: €490 vs €1,500 vs €7,500; pilot model: CAC vs full-price). Having more debate does not resolve the contradictions — it adds more positions to argue between.

What Alizé needs is not a better debate. It is a decision made by Louis about his own time, a map of his actual network, and one real conversation with a human who might pay money.

If Pulse 32 runs before U91/U52 are resolved and before Louis has sent one outreach message, Pulse 32 is not research. It is avoidance.
