## Debate 1: Pricing — The €7,500 Warm Pilot Is Unsellable

**Agent:** Pricing Viability Debate

### Core Challenge
D65 and D72 assume warm contacts = committed buyers, and that €7,500 is the right price for warm network pilots. Both assumptions fail under scrutiny:

1. **Warm contacts are accessible, not converted.** A warm contact answers the phone. A committed buyer signs a PO. They are not the same person. The €7,500 price assumes the latter when you often only have the former.

2. **D107 confirmed: price unsellable regardless of who closes.** Even a senior commercial co-founder faces the same objection: €7,500 for an unknown brand with zero references. The closer handles the objection more gracefully — but doesn't eliminate it.

3. **First pilot buyers provide maximum leverage to Alizé and bear maximum risk.** They should pay less, not the same or more.

### The Math
| Scenario | Price | Prob. Close | Expected Value | Case Studies |
|----------|-------|-------------|----------------|--------------|
| €7,500 pilots (D65/D72) | €22,500 | Near zero | ~€0–2,000 | 0 |
| €1,500 pilots (CAC model) | €4,500 | ~90% | ~€1,500 | 3 |

The €7,500 approach has theoretical upside €22,500 but near-zero capture probability. The €1,500 approach captures ~€1,500 and generates the references that unlock €7,500 pricing for pilots 4–10.

### Challenge to D90
D90 blocks warm outreach until a closing-capable person exists. But a senior closer faces the same price objection. Adding polish doesn't solve a product-market fit problem.

### Recommendation
- **Phase 1 (Pilots 1–3):** €1,500–2,000 with full case study rights + testimonial + logo use
- **Phase 2 (Pilots 4–6):** €3,500–5,000 as "early partner rate"
- **Phase 3 (Pilots 7+):** €7,500 with reference deck from 3–6 previous clients

### New Decisions
- **D110:** First 3 pilots priced at €1,500–2,000 as CAC investment, not margin play
- **D111:** Case study rights + testimonial in exchange for reduced price — explicit commercial term
- **D112:** €7,500 pricing unlocked only after 3+ reference clients exist

## Debate 2: Delivery Partner — Louis Delivers First, Then Finds Partner

**Agent:** Delivery Partner Chicken-and-Egg Agent

### Core Challenge
D48 requires finding a delivery partner BEFORE the first pilot. D109 says Louis cannot evaluate delivery quality he cannot perform. These are circular. You cannot vet a capability you have never exercised.

### The Deadlock
```
D48: Find delivery partner before pilot 1
    ↓
D109: Louis cannot evaluate whether partner is qualified
    ↓
D111: Louis needs delivery education before Month 1
    ↓
Circular — you can't know what delivery education means without doing delivery
```

### Evidence
- D104–D106 disqualified the web agency as rehearsal proxy (scale mismatch)
- D99–D103 creates false choice between "internal rehearsal" and "external pilot"
- The "delivery rehearsal" framing is an excuse to defer real client work

### The Correct Sequence
1. **Month 1, Louis solo:** Ship a stripped-down pilot (ONE workflow, ONE integration, ONE client contact). Louis learns delivery by doing it. Documents what broke and what worked.
2. **Month 2, Louis + evaluation:** Louis uses his own delivery experience as evaluation criteria. Screens delivery partners. Runs pilot 2 with Louis still lead.
3. **Month 3, contractor onboarding:** Louis selects contractor based on Month 2 evaluation. Co-delivers pilot 3. Steps back from execution.

### Challenge to D99
"Internal delivery rehearsal" is not a separate activity from delivery. The first pilot IS delivery learning if scoped correctly (small scope, Louis-only, real client or simulated).

### New Decisions
- **D113:** Louis delivers pilot 1 solo — this IS the delivery education, not a prelude to it
- **D114:** Find delivery partner AFTER pilot 1 complete, using Louis's first-hand evaluation criteria
- **D115:** Month 1 pilot scoped to single workflow, single integration — enough to learn from, too small to fail catastrophically
- **D116:** Delivery partner is a Month 2+ decision, not a prerequisite to starting

## Debate 3: GTM Prerequisites — The Clock Hasn't Started

**Agent:** GTM Prerequisites Collapse Agent

### Core Challenge
The "3–6 month effective window" (D82) is a fiction. The clock requires four things to be true before it starts ticking:
1. Louis has quantified and ring-fenced hours (U91 — unresolved)
2. Closing-capable person exists (U86 — unresolved)
3. Grinto conflict contractually resolved (U87 — unresolved)
4. Warm network ICP mapped (U88 — unresolved)

U86 (commercial co-founder recruitment) takes 4–8 weeks alone. U87 (Grinto notice period) takes 1–3 months. These prerequisites have not started being resolved.

**The realistic effective window start date: Month 3–4 at earliest.**

### Challenge to D82
D82's 3–6 month window is more accurately: 2–3 months of execution preceded by 3–4 months of prerequisite resolution. The plan confounds setup with execution.

### Challenge to D87 (Network Narrative Risk)
D87 says 3–6 months of failed closes creates a permanent bad network narrative. This is a real risk — but it only applies if outreach starts and fails. The risk is avoided by not starting badly, not by waiting indefinitely.

### Challenge to D90
Warm outreach can proceed with honest framing: "I'm working on something new, not ready to pitch yet." 10–15 conversations with top warmest accounts provide market feedback without a formal sales cycle.

### Challenge to D91
25 hours/week set as threshold without U91 data. If Louis can only commit 15, the plan needs to adjust — not pretend 25 is required.

### The Real Month 1–3 (Honest Version)

| Phase | Activities | Output |
|-------|------------|--------|
| **Prerequisite Sprint (M1 W1–2)** | Track hours. Legal Grinto review. Identify 3–5 co-founder candidates. Map top 20 warm contacts. | Confirmed hours baseline. Grinto risk assessment. Co-founder shortlist. |
| **Parallel Build (M1 W3–4 + M2)** | Co-founder conversations. 5–10 warm insight-gathering calls (not pitches). Positioning refinement. | Co-founder fit signals. ICP validated with real feedback. |
| **Warm Initiation (M3)** | First calls with top 10 warmest accounts. Louis leads with personal credibility. Co-founder offer extended. | 5–10 conversations. 2–3 expressed needs. Co-founder decision. |

### New Decisions
- **D117:** 3–6 month effective window (D82) starts when warm outreach produces qualified pipeline — not when planning begins
- **D118:** Month 1 relabeled as "Prerequisite Resolution + Warm-Up" — honest about what can actually happen
- **D119:** Grinto ring-fencing explored in Week 1; if viable, removes 1–3 month delay
- **D120:** Warm outreach proceeds in Month 1–2 under honest framing (Louis as operator, not formal Alizé pitch) with narrow ICP (top 10–15 warmest accounts)
- **D121:** D91's 25 hr/week threshold reclassified as aspirational until U91 provides actual data
