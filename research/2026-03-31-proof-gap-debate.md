## Question 3: Is Kuroba's Operational Deployment a Legitimate Proof Point?

### The Case FOR Kuroba as Proof (D194/D195)

- **Named co-founders, verifiable relationship:** Maëli and Gabin exist, can be contacted, and their endorsement is verifiable. This is better than an anonymized case study because it is falsifiable.
- **"We eat our own cooking":** The oldest credibility move in B2B. Louis is running his own business on Alizé. This is honest and specific.
- **Real workflow:** Kuroba's client communication workflow is a genuine use case. It exists. It is not a demo.
- **Fastest path to something real:** Deploying Kuroba takes days, not months. The moment it is live, Louis has a proof point.

### The Case AGAINST Kuroba as Proof (D241/D242)

- **Scale mismatch:** 2-3 person web agency ≠ 50-200 person service firm. A prospect at a 100-person company can reasonably dismiss "well it worked for a 3-person agency."
- **Workflow mismatch:** Kuroba runs web agency workflows. Alizé's claimed ICP is professional services, back-office admin. The workflows are different enough that Kuroba proof doesn't directly validate the claimed use case.
- **Self-interest credibility problem:** Louis co-founded Kuroba. A prospect might reasonably ask: "Of course you say it works — it's your own company."
- **Not yet live:** Currently "in progress" per D194. Until it is running and demonstrable, it is a plan, not proof.

### Verdict on Kuroba as Proof Point

**Kuroba is a legitimate proof point for warm outreach to Louis's direct network — NOT for cold outbound to strangers.**

The reasoning:
- For warm outreach (to people who know Louis/Maëli/Gabin): Kuroba is strong. The relationship provides the trust that scale and workflow gaps undermine in cold contexts.
- For cold outbound: Kuroba is weak. The scale mismatch and self-interest problem require a prospect to take Louis's word against their own judgment of fit.
- For demo purposes: Kuroba is excellent. A screen recording of a live agent working is compelling regardless of the audience. It is proof of capability, not proof of market fit.

**Practical recommendation:** Use Kuroba as the demo asset (Loom, screen share). Do not use it as the social proof claim in cold outreach. In warm outreach to people who know the Kuroba founders, use it as social proof.

## Recommendation: Should Outreach Wait for Proof, or Begin Immediately?

**Begin immediately — with honest framing.**

### The Case for Immediate Outreach

1. **The proof required for first contact is not case studies.** First contact only needs to generate a response. A message that says "I'm building AI agents for French businesses, we've deployed our first one at our own agency, want to see how it works?" is sufficient.

2. **Discovery conversations are the proof mechanism.** D235 says "discovery calls are the positioning refinement mechanism." The best proof of what Alizé can sell is a prospect telling you what they need. This requires talking to prospects, not waiting for case studies.

3. **The minimum viable credibility package is almost entirely non-technical.** Louis's bio, a one-paragraph explanation, 5 LinkedIn messages. None of this requires case studies.

4. **Warm outreach doesn't require proof — it requires relationship.** D51 is explicit: warm outreach (LinkedIn to known contacts) starts Day 1 and is NOT gated by website or case studies. Louis's warm contacts already know him. They don't need Alizé to have 3 case studies before responding to a LinkedIn message.

5. **Cold outreach is gated correctly.** D47 correctly gates cold outbound (unknown brand, no relationship) behind website + pilot + ICP + delivery. This is the right standard. Warm outreach is not held to this standard.

### What "Begin Immediately" Means

- Send 5 LinkedIn messages TODAY (D197 — unresolved since Pulse 38)
- Deploy Kuroba agent THIS WEEK (D194 — in progress)
- Record a 3-minute Loom demo THIS WEEK
- Buy alize.studio domain TODAY (U163 — unresolved for 45+ pulses)

### What "Begin Immediately" Does NOT Mean

- Do not claim client results you don't have
- Do not name specific ROI metrics without measurement
- Do not pitch Alizé as if it has dozens of happy clients
- Do not cold email prospects with a fake case study

### The Actual Risk of Waiting

The debate log shows 48 pulses producing 200+ decisions and zero execution. The risk of waiting is not "we'll launch with imperfect proof." The risk is that 48 more pulses produce another decision document and still zero clients.

**Proof does not open doors. Conversations open doors. Proof confirms the conversation was worth having.**

The first outreach message does not need to prove Alizé works. It only needs to generate enough interest that a 15-minute call happens. After that call, a demo happens. After the demo, pricing is discussed. After delivery, proof exists. The sequence is: conversation → demo → payment → proof. Not: proof → conversation → demo → payment.
