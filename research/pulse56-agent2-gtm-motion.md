## What the Research Hasn't Considered

**The "warm network" framing has never been tested as what it actually is — a social proof generator, not a sales channel.**

D172 frames warm network as a revenue source ("€1,500 fixed-fee projects"). D173 frames warm referrals as the growth flywheel ("cold outreach → delivery → warm referrals"). But the warm network's real function is generating external evidence (case studies, testimonials, named references) that makes ALL OTHER CHANNELS work.

**If warm network = social proof factory instead of sales channel, the entire GTM sequence inverts:**

Current: Louis reaches out → attempts to close → delivers → hopes for referral

Corrected: Louis builds working agent → publishes demo/template → warm network consumes it and opts in → delivery happens → referrals follow

**The research hasn't seriously modeled:**
1. What Alizé looks like as an inbound-first, productized service (templates, playbooks, working demos accessible without a sales call)
2. Whether D172's "€1,500 fixed-fee project" can work as a self-serve purchase (landing page → buy → deliver) rather than a sold deal
3. Whether P54-D2's "self-serve bridge" should be the PRIMARY motion, not the bridge

## What a "No Human Outreach Year 1" Alizé Looks Like

**Pipeline sources (in priority order):**

1. **Published working agents (free tier):** Louis builds 2-3 working agent templates for expert-comptables (D126 beachhead: invoice processing, regulatory reporting, client onboarding). Publishes them publicly. Prospects self-evaluate.

2. **Productized €1,500 purchase page:** Landing page → "Buy this workflow automation" → credit card → discovery call scheduled (not sales call). Removes Louis from the close entirely.

3. **Demo video + waitlist:** Not "book a call" — "watch how it works + join waitlist for early access." Nurture sequence kicks in. Only then does human enter.

4. **Warm network AS DISTRIBUTION, not sales:** Send warm contacts the published template/demo, not a pitch. "Look what we built" not "would you be interested."

5. **Malt.fr as review-accumulation engine (D136 confirmed):** After first self-serve clients, reviews accumulate. Passive inbound via search.

**What changes structurally:**
- Louis's role shifts from "salesperson" to "product builder + content publisher"
- Pipeline generation is automated/digital, not human-mediated
- Conversion happens before human contact, not after
- Warm network amplifies published work, not the other way around
