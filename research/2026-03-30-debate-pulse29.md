## Challenge 1: Expert-Comptable Vertical Thesis

**Challenging D126, D135, D81REVISED**

**Legs of the thesis:**
1. Governance gap disqualifies Copilot for expert-comptables
2. Regulatory workflows provide clear case study metrics
3. Louis's network has connections here

**Key challenges:**

### Governance Wedge Overstated
D127 already conceded governance gap is "overstated for 70% of ICP." But D126 doubles down on expert-comptables as the 30% without explaining why. French expert-comptable firms range from 1-5 person boutiques to 50+ person firms — governance requirements are not uniform. Copilot's data residency limitations are enterprise-grade problems, not boutique problems. The EU AI Act compliance story is 2027-2028 (D42), not a present differentiator. Expert-comptables have used Microsoft tools for 15+ years; governance anxiety is lower, not higher.

**Verdict:** Governance can justify positioning Alizé as governance-aware. It does NOT justify expert-comptables as a specific vertical beachhead because of governance. The argument was assembled after the vertical was chosen.

### Workflow Still Not Specific
D81REVISED says "expert-comptable administrative workflow" — but this describes 40% of what an expert-comptable does, not a specific task. A real first workflow requires: (a) measurable ROI in 30 days, (b) no sensitive client data exposure, (c) clear case study value. Expert-comptables touch client financial data for tax returns, payroll, bilan, TVA declarations — the "no sensitive data" requirement eliminates 90% of actual work. The remaining 10% (internal scheduling, team coordination) is operationally trivial with negligible ROI.

**Verdict:** D81REVISED is not a workflow choice — it is a placeholder. Without naming the exact task (e.g., "TVA declaration preparation from extracted source documents" or "client document collection status tracking"), the case study thesis collapses.

### Network Dependency = Premature Lock-In
D135 modified D126 to "go where Grinto relationships exist first." This is honest but reveals the thesis: the vertical is expert-comptables because of network access, not because it is optimal. U123 and U129 are still unresolved. If Louis has <5 expert-comptable warm contacts, the vertical requires cold outreach that D47 blockers prevent until Month 3-4+.

**Verdict:** The vertical was locked before the network was mapped. This is the most consequential sequence error in 29 pulses of research.

### Domain Knowledge Gap
D125 sets €1,500/2-week diagnostic. Without domain knowledge (U124 unresolved), Louis cannot credibly identify the right workflow, scope it correctly, or evaluate whether the solution is actually working. A mis-scoped first diagnostic = a failed first pilot = no case study.

## Challenge 2: Pricing Architecture

**Challenging D125, D43, D72, D110, D123**

### The €1,500 Diagnostic Lands in the "Too Cheap to Trust" Gap

At €1,500, Alizé is simultaneously too expensive for an unknown brand (no credibility) and too cheap to signal quality (below the "serious investment" threshold). The gap:

| Price Point | Buyer Perception |
|-------------|-----------------|
| €0 | Desperation, no skin in the game, maximum free-rider |
| €1,000-1,500 | "Risky" — skin in game but unclear ROI, high dropout |
| €2,500-3,000 | Premium signal — requires credibility Alizé doesn't have yet |

**Louis's actual time cost:** €200/day × 5 days = €1,000. At €1,500, Louis nets €500 after covering his time cost — insufficient margin to sustain a quality diagnostic.

**Recommendation:** FREE 30-min discovery call + €490 written deliverable credited toward pilot. The in-person interaction is free (removes friction), the written deliverable adds perceived value without requiring cash from an unknown brand.

### The Tiered Monthly Model Fails the Budget Conversation

At €1,200-2,500/month, Alizé enters a budget conversation with French PME buyers who have no discretionary AI line item. Every euro comes from an existing operating budget. The tiered pricing creates "which tier do I need?" confusion — exactly the decision paralysis that leads to no decision.

**The real budget test:** "We propose allocating €1,500/month from our operations budget to automate X tasks." This conversation requires the DG to justify the allocation against other priorities. Tiered pricing multiplies the complexity of that justification.

**Recommendation:** Single price, single framing. Not "pick your tier" — "here's what it costs to fix your specific problem." Price the outcome, not the feature set.

### CAC Pilot Model Has Unknown Sustainability

At €1,500-2,000 for pilots 1-3, Louis is effectively paying to acquire customers. Louis's time at €200/day = €1,000-1,200 for a 2-week diagnostic. Net margin per diagnostic: -€500 to -€1,000 (Louis subsidized). The question is: how many subsidized pilots can Louis personally fund before Alizé runs out of money?

**Unit economics reality:** At €1,500 diagnostic with -€500 margin, 10 subsidized pilots = -€5,000 cash out. This is acceptable as CAC only if: (a) conversion rate from diagnostic to retainer is high enough to make this back, and (b) Louis has the capital to sustain it. Neither has been validated.

**Recommendation:** FREE diagnostic (qualification only, no deliverable). Pilot at €7,500 (full cost recovery). Retainer at €2,000-2,500/month. The funnel is: free call → paid pilot → recurring retainer. The diagnostic must be free to maximize funnel top.

## Challenge 3: GTM Velocity

**Challenging D82, D120, D130, D138, D47, U131**

### Warm Network Math = Planning Fiction Until U52 Resolves

U52 (Louis's network ICP mapping) has been unresolved for 28 consecutive pulses. Twenty-eight. D138 says "warm outreach starts Month 1" but the contact list has never been validated. D135 effectively concedes the vertical is network-dependent — but the network has not been mapped.

**The contingency that doesn't exist:** What if Louis's warm network has <10 ICP-fit contacts (defined as: 20-50 person firm, decision-maker access, relevant sector)? The plan has optimism, not a contingency.

**Verdict:** Warm outreach cannot be a planned primary channel until U52 returns a number. If <10 ICP-fit contacts, warm outreach is a hope, not a channel.

### The Case Study Gate Collapses the Cold Outbound Window

D47 says cold outbound needs: live website + 1 completed pilot + ICP confirmed + delivery confirmed. D101 says first external paying client is Month 2. Earliest realistic cold outbound start: Month 3 at best — and that's assuming zero slippage.

D82 says the effective window is 3-6 months. If cold outbound starts at Month 3, the effective cold outbound window is Month 3-6: 3 months. If pilot slips to Month 3 completion, cold outbound starts at Month 4 and the window collapses to 2 months.

**The internal inconsistency:** D130 mentions Malt + association outreach as supplements — neither requires a completed case study to start. The case study gate is selectively enforced. This suggests the gate is a story to justify cold outbound delay, not a hard constraint.

### The "3-6 Month Window" Is a Capacity Constraint, Not a Market Event

D82 establishes a 3-6 month "effective window." But D76 set first-mover window at 6-18 months, and Microsoft Copilot threat is 18-36 months out (D12). Nothing in the market changes at Month 6.

What actually changes at Month 6 is: Louis's capital runs out, or his network patience wears out, or his other commitments become incompatible with continued Alizé investment. These are personal exhaustion points, not market events.

**The framing matters:** Calling it a "market window" creates false urgency about competitors. Calling it a "capacity constraint" creates the right urgency about Louis's own sustainability.

### Association Outreach Is Month 9+ Disguised as Month 1-3 Supplement

U131 asks which expert-comptable body to approach. The major bodies (CSN, AGA) have decision timelines of 6-18 months for any formal endorsement. Individual relationship-building with regional bodies takes 3-6 months minimum before a single referral is generated.

**Verdict:** Association outreach is a Month 9+ channel. Treating it as a Month 1-3 supplement is optimistic to the point of being dishonest.

## Cross-Debate Finding: The Sequence Error at the Center

The most dangerous unresolved item — U52 (Louis's ICP network mapping) — has been listed in 28 consecutive pulses without resolution. Every decision downstream of it (vertical choice, pricing tier, GTM channel) is built on an assumption about Louis's network that has never been confirmed.
