## Agent 2: Unit Economics — "€2,000/Month Does Not Work"

**Question:** Can the €2,000/month retainer sustain a business where each pilot requires 10-15 delivery days at €400-450/day?

**Assumption challenged:** "€2,000/month is the right price point"

**Verdict: The math produces a structural loss. The pricing appears to be internally convenient, not market-validated.**

### The Core Contradiction

| Decision | Pilot Fee | Monthly Retainer |
|----------|-----------|-----------------|
| D72 | €7,500 warm network | — |
| D110 | €1,500-2,000 | — |
| D43 | — | €2,000-2,500 |
| D125 | €1,500/2-week diagnostic | — |

**D72 and D110 cannot both be true.** You cannot have a €1,500 CAC model and a €7,500 warm network price for equivalent work.

### Unit Economics (Base Assumptions)

**Month 1: Pilot Phase**
```
Revenue:  Pilot fee  €1,500
Costs:    Delivery (12.5 days × €425 avg)  €5,313
          Louis (3 days × €200)             €600
NET:                                     -€4,413
```

**Months 2-3: Ongoing Delivery**
```
Monthly Revenue:  €2,000 retainer
Monthly Costs:   Louis (2 days × €200)  €400
                 Delivery (embedded)      €0-400
Monthly Net:                         €1,200-1,600
```

**3-Month Client Economics (Base):**
- Total Revenue: €5,500
- Total Costs: €6,713
- **NET: -€1,213 per client**

The business loses €350-€3,250 per client in the first 3 months depending on assumptions.

### What Would Fix It

The business needs ONE of:
1. **Raise pilot fee to €5,000-7,500** (covers delivery cost + Louis time + margin)
2. **Raise retainer to €3,500-4,500/month** (recoups pilot loss over 3 months)
3. **Reduce delivery days to 4-5 per pilot** (requires radically reduced scope)
4. **Win only "warm network" clients at €7,500/pilot** (scales poorly)
5. **Subsidized delivery partner** (€150-200/day, unrealistic for qualified PME consultant)

### Where Did €2,000 Come From?

No market research is cited. No competitive analysis is cited. No cost-plus calculation is cited. **This is internal convenience pricing.**

At cost-plus:
```
Louis monthly cost (20 days × €200):   €4,000
Delivery costs (5 days × €425):        €2,125
Overhead (30%):                        €1,838
-------------------------------------------
Minimum viable price:                   €7,963/month
```

The €2,000 price is 25% of cost-plus.

### Brutal Recommendations

1. **Kill D110 (€1,500 pilot) immediately** — this is a charity model, not a CAC model
2. **Raise pilot fee to €5,000-7,500** — this makes the pilot break-even
3. **Raise retainer to €3,500-4,500/month** — gives margin for ongoing delivery
4. **Get market validation** — talk to 5 potential clients and ask what they'd pay
