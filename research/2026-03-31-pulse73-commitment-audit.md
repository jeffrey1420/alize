## 1. What the Commitment Pattern Actually Is

The pattern is **decision-as-terminal-event**. Louis treats a decision as completed when it is made in the pulse log, not when it is enforced in reality. This is not procrastination, laziness, or poor time management. It is a specific structural failure: the pulse process converts obligations into artifacts, and artifacts feel like accomplishment.

**Specific evidence:**

- **U163 (buy domain, €10):** Called "€10, 5 minutes, no research required." Unresolved for 72+ pulses. The research apparatus has produced 72+ separate findings that the domain should be bought today. The domain has not been bought.

- **Outreach messages:** Drafted across multiple pulses. Zero messages sent. The drafting is logged as productive activity. The non-sending is invisible in the pulse log.

- **Kuroba deployment:** Called "18 pulses overdue" in Pulse 55 (per prior audit notes). Still not done by Pulse 72. The gap between "overdue" and "done" never closes. "Overdue" is a descriptive tag, not a corrective mechanism.

- **U91 (binary choice: startup vs side-project):** Unresolved after 91+ days. An explicit April 1 deadline was set (D196). The deadline passed. The binary remains unresolved. The log records the deadline, notes the miss, and moves to the next research item.

- **D223 (weekly behavioral commitment tracker):** Proposed. Never implemented. The proposal exists in the decision log. The tracker does not exist.

- **Four prior pulse suspension decisions (D249, D258, D213, D240):** All failed to stop the process. The process generates the decision to suspend itself, then continues. A system that can decide to suspend itself but does not has not decided to suspend itself — it has performed the theater of deciding.

**The pattern:** Research generates decisions. Decisions generate TODO items. TODO items generate more research. External reality — where domains cost money and messages require send buttons — remains unchanged.

## 2. Whether Any Research Output Can Change This Pattern

**Answer: No. The mechanism that creates the problem is the same mechanism tasked with solving it.**

The pulse process is research. Research produces documents. Documents are internal artifacts. External execution requires actions that leave the document system. The entire apparatus is structurally incapable of producing external action because the apparatus IS the document system.

Evidence this is structural, not circumstantial:

- Research produced D91 (25+ hours/week gate). D91 has never been measured. Research produced D221 (behavioral binary as replacement). D221 has never been implemented. The research process identifies its own failures and generates replacements — which also fail — without ever exiting the research loop.

- The domain-buying task (U163) has survived 72+ confrontations with its own simplicity. "€10, 5 minutes, no research required" appears in the pulse log. The task has been categorized as "no research required" for 72+ pulses. This means the research apparatus correctly identified that no more research is needed. The task remains unresolved. The research apparatus has already confirmed it cannot help. It continues anyway.

- U91 has survived 91 days. The binary question "startup vs side-project" does not require market research. It requires Louis to state a preference and commit to its consequences. The research apparatus keeps generating frameworks, decision trees, and capacity analyses to defer the moment of statement.

**The research apparatus will continue producing findings until the domain name is bought or Louis explicitly terminates the process.** There is no finding that will cause Louis to buy the domain. There is no market insight that resolves the startup/side-project binary. These are action tasks masquerading as research tasks, and the research process cannot unmasks itself.

## 3. What Would Change the Execution Rate

**The honest answer: probably nothing within the current structure.**

If forced to identify interventions that could theoretically move the needle, ordered by plausibility:

**Intervention 1: Remove the decision from the document system.**
The TODO.md and decision log are the problem. They are internal reward signals. When a decision appears in the log, Louis experiences it as accomplished. The intervention: stop logging decisions as completions. Only count external actions — sent emails, bought domains, signed contracts, deployed systems. Every pulse session ends with a count of external actions taken, not decisions made.

**Intervention 2: Named accountability with real consequences.**
The prior commitment tracker (D223) failed because it had no teeth. If Louis sends a weekly 5-sentence report to one named person who has agreed to impose a specific consequence for zero-delivery weeks (ending the venture, stopping the process, alerting co-founders), the structure changes. The consequence must be named in advance, agreed to explicitly, and applied without negotiation when triggered. Without named consequences, tracking is theater.

**Intervention 3: Kill the process or change its owner.**
The pulse process cannot terminate itself (4 failed suspension attempts). Someone else must disable it. This could be Maëli, Gabin, or an external party. The process continues because Louis is the only actor in the system and Louis's incentives favor continuation (research feels productive, action risks failure). A co-founder or trusted advisor with authority to kill the process forces a real choice: execute without the research safety blanket, or stop.

**Intervention 4: Force the U91 binary explicitly.**
U91 "startup vs side-project" survives because its consequences are abstract. State them plainly: if startup, Louis reduces Grinto hours or exits the web agency by a specific date. If side-project, Alizé's scope collapses to 1-2 pilots in Year 1 with no external hires, no delivery partner, and no infrastructure investment. The binary must carry explicit, uncomfortable, named consequences for each branch. Without the consequence statement, the binary is just a question.

**What will NOT change execution:**
- More research (demonstrated: 72+ pulses of research produced 0 external actions)
- Better TODO tracking (demonstrated: TODO.md has been current and comprehensive for the entire run; execution is unchanged)
- Deadline setting (demonstrated: April 1 deadline for U91 passed; binary unresolved)
- Suspension attempts by the process itself (demonstrated: 4 failures)
- Commitment trackers without named consequences (demonstrated: D223 proposed, never built)

## 4. Conclusion: Can Alizé as Currently Structured Execute?

**No. Not as currently structured. Not without external intervention.**

The pulse process is not a productivity system. It is a deferral system with a productivity aesthetic. It has produced 270+ decisions, a comprehensive TODO, a brand document, a technical architecture, and zero external actions after 72+ pulses. The correlation between research output and external action is zero. The correlation between decision count and execution is zero. The correlation between TODO item count and done items is zero.

The prior audit (AUDIT-EXECUTION-ARCHITECTURE.md, Pulse 58) reached similar conclusions via architectural analysis. This audit reaches the same conclusion via behavioral evidence. Both point to the same root cause: **the system Louis built to execute produces research, and research does not execute.**

Three conditions would need to be met for Alizé to execute in its current form:

1. **The pulse process must be terminated or fundamentally restructured** to count only external actions, not decisions. This cannot happen within the current process.

2. **Louis must resolve U91** (startup vs side-project) with explicit, uncomfortable consequences named for each branch. Ambiguity must be eliminated by statement, not by more research.

3. **Named accountability with teeth must exist.** Not a tracker. Not a weekly report. A named person who has agreed to a specific consequence if the weekly report shows zero external actions.

Until all three conditions are met simultaneously, the next pulse will produce more decisions, more TODO items, and zero external actions. This is not a prediction — it is the pattern confirmed across 72 pulses.

**What this audit found:** The commitment problem is not that Louis doesn't know what to do. The commitment problem is that knowing what to do, and documenting knowing what to do, has become a substitute for doing it. The pulse process is the mechanism of substitution. It will continue until something outside it stops it.
