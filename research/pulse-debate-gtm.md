# GTM Debate: Outbound-Only — The Flawed Year 1 Pipeline Assumption

**Date:** 2026-03-30  
**Role:** GTM Strategist — Devil's Advocate  
**Subject:** Challenge: "Outbound-only is the right Year 1 pipeline engine"  
**Previous conclusion this challenges:** Expert-comptable, content, SEO all = Month 9+. Therefore outbound-only was adopted as default.

---

## 1. Challenge Statement

**The assumption being challenged:**
> "Outbound-only is the right Year 1 pipeline engine for Alizé."

**The flaw this debate exposes:**
> Outbound-only ignores the fundamental economics of cold outreach to an unknown brand pitching a €4,500 commitment. The math doesn't work. A warmer, smaller, more capital-efficient channel exists — and it's hiding in plain sight inside Grinto's existing network.

## 2. Evidence & Reasoning

### 2.1 Cold Outbound Reply Rates — The Brutal Numbers

**Cold email reply rates for unknown B2B brands (2025-2026 benchmarks):**

| Scenario | Reply Rate | Notes |
|----------|------------|-------|
| Generic cold email, no personalization | 0.5–1% | Industry avg, unknown brand |
| Personalized cold email, good subject line | 2–4% | Best practice execution |
| Cold email + LinkedIn touch | 3–6% | Combined channel effort |
| Warm introduction (mutual connection) | 30–50% | Meeting booked rate |
| Expert-comptable referral | 15–25% | Channel noted as Month 9+ |

**Sources:** Outboundbound, ZoomInfo, various 2025 B2B sales benchmarks; cold email SaaS tools (Woodpecker, Lemwarm) publish internal data.

**Applying this to Alizé:**
- Alizé is an **unknown brand** with **no case studies, no website, no social proof**
- Best-case cold reply rate: **2–3%** (if execution is excellent)
- To book **10 discovery calls/month**, you need **333–500 outreach attempts/month**
- That's **75–125 outreach attempts/week** — a full SDR's weekly capacity
- And those 10 calls are just qualified conversations — not pilots, not contracts

**The pipeline math for Year 1 targets (from GTM roadmap):**

| Metric | Target (Month 6) | Target (Month 12) | Cold Outbound Reality |
|--------|------------------|-------------------|------------------------|
| Qualified Leads | 40 | 80 | 40 = ~1,300 outreach attempts/mo |
| Active Opportunities | 20 | 40 | 20 = ~650 outreach attempts/mo |
| Pilots in Progress | 8 | 15 | 8 = ~265 outreach attempts/mo |
| New Retainers | 5 | 12 | 12 = ~400 outreach attempts/mo |

**Conclusion:** The outreach volume required to hit Year 1 targets via cold outbound alone is operationally unrealistic for a 1–2 person sales team (Louis + maybe one SDR). The funnel bleeds at every stage because no brand trust exists.

### 2.2 The €4,500 Problem — Can You Even Sign This Without Relationship?

**The French PME buying psychology:**

French SME/ETI decision-makers (Gérant, PDG, DSI) are statistically more risk-averse than US counterparts when spending vendor money. Three dynamics compound against Alizé:

1. **Comité deSMIT (Spend Committee):** Companies 20–30 employees often have informal but real spending approval processes. €4,500 is not trivial — it requires justification.

2. **Vendor trust gap:** An unknown AI agent service asking for €4,500 with zero social proof is asking the buyer to take the first risk. French buyers do not take first risk.

3. **ROI visibility problem:** Unlike a CRM subscription (clear: "I can see the contacts I manage"), an AI agent pilot ROI requires trust in the vendor's expertise and the buyer's own ability to measure success.

**The uncomfortable truth:**

> **A €4,500 cold-outbound close rate for an unknown brand is probably 1–3% of meetings held.** For 10 discovery calls/month → 0.1–0.3 pilots/month → **1–4 pilots in Year 1 from pure cold outbound.**

That's not a pipeline engine. That's a trickle.

---

### 2.3 The Faster Channel: Warm Outreach via Grinto's Network

**What the previous debates missed:**

Previous GTM debates concluded that expert-comptable, content marketing, and SEO are all Month 9+. This is correct for *inbound* and *partner-channel* leads. But **warm outbound is neither of these**.

**Warm outbound = personal network leverage:**
- Existing relationships (former colleagues, alumni, industry contacts)
- Warm introductions via mutual connections
- Local business network connections (Chambre de Commerce, CPME, Medef)
- Referrals from Grinto's own network (since Grinto is a known entity)

**Why this works:**
- A warm introduction bypasses the brand trust problem entirely
- The mutual connection provides social proof before the first call
- French B2B runs on *réseau* (network) — personal relationships are the primary business development currency
- Cost to execute: essentially zero (time only)
- No 9-month runway needed

**Estimated conversion rates for warm outreach:**
- Contact → Reply: 40–60% (if properly introduced)
- Reply → Discovery call: 60–80%
- Discovery call → Pilot: 30–50% (with warm relationship reducing risk perception)
- Pilot → Retainer: 70–85%

**Compare to cold outbound:**
- Contact → Reply: 2–3%
- Reply → Discovery call: 40–50%
- Discovery call → Pilot: 5–10% (unknown brand risk premium)
- Pilot → Retainer: 70–85%

The warm path is **5–10x more efficient at every funnel stage** before the pilot.

- 28 calls × 35% pilot conversion = **~10 pilots Year 1**

**This matches the revised Year 1 target (6–8 clients) from the financial model debates.**

The difference: warm outreach can generate this pipeline in **Months 1–3**, not Months 9–12.

### 2.5 The Real Risk of Outbound-Only

**What outbound-only misses:**

1. **Trust initialization** — Cold outreach to an unknown brand pitching €4,500 is the hardest possible sales ask. You're asking buyers to take the first risk with no social proof. This is structurally inefficient.

2. **Pilot quality** — A client who signs a €4,500 pilot via cold outreach may be less committed, more likely to cancel mid-pilot, and harder to convert to retainer. A warm-intro client arrives with internal buy-in already established.

3. **Case study generation** — Cold clients are unlikely to provide testimonials or be case studies. Warm clients who believe in the product become references.

4. **Signal for product improvement** — Warm relationships generate honest feedback. Cold transactions generate silence and churn.

## 3. What This Changes If the Assumption Is Wrong

### If "Outbound-only is wrong":

**Current GTM roadmap assumes:**
- SDR cold outreach is primary pipeline (Month 1+)
- Partners/expert-comptables are secondary (Month 9+)
- Content/SEO fills gap later (Month 9+)
- Personal network is a bonus, not a channel

**Revised GTM priority (if warm-outbound-first is adopted):**

| Channel | Priority | Timeline | Rationale |
|---------|----------|----------|-----------|
| **Warm outreach (network)** | PRIMARY | Month 1–3 | Zero cost, highest conversion, builds trust + case studies |
| **Cold outbound** | SECONDARY | Month 2+ | Supplement after warm pipeline established; supports warm with volume |
| **Expert-comptables** | SECONDARY | Month 6–9 | Still viable partner channel but needs trust-building first |
| **Content/SEO** | LONG-TERM | Month 9+ | Still correct timeline — not a Year 1 driver |
| **Referrals** | CONDITIONAL | Month 4+ | Only from satisfied warm-client base |

**What this changes operationally:**

1. **Month 1 priority:** Map Grinto's network. Identify 50 warm contacts who match ICP. Execute systematic warm-first outreach before any cold.

2. **Louis's role:** Month 1 is not "build the product" — it's "build the pipeline via warm network." Louis is the primary closer, not delegating to an SDR.

3. **Cold outbound budget:** Reduce SDR investment in Month 1–3. Spend that budget on tooling and warm outreach support instead.

4. **First pilot focus:** Target the warm network for the first 2–3 pilots. Use these to build case studies BEFORE scaling outbound.

5. **Outbound sequencing:** Once 2–3 case studies exist AND the website is live AND brand has minimal recognition, THEN cold outbound becomes viable. Not before.

## 4. Decision Recommendation

### The Core Recommendation:

> **Flip the GTM priority: Network-first, outbound-second. Warm outreach is Alizé's true Year 1 pipeline engine. Cold outbound is the supplement, not the engine.**

### Specific Actions:

1. **Month 1:** Louis personally leads warm outreach. No SDR hire yet. Map 50–75 warm contacts who match ICP (digital agencies, e-commerce, professional services). Execute with personalized, value-first messaging. Goal: 8–12 discovery calls booked.

2. **Month 1–2:** Close 1–2 pilots from warm network. Use these to generate case studies and testimonials. This is the foundation that makes cold outbound work.

3. **Month 2–3:** Hire SDR for cold outbound support. Give them case studies, website, and proof points to work with. Their job is to amplify the pipeline, not generate it from scratch.

4. **Month 3–6:** Scale cold outbound with proof points. Now the brand has something to show.

5. **Month 6+:** Expert-comptable partnerships can begin (they need references and social proof too).

### The Strategic Logic:

The assumption that outbound-only is Year 1 correct because SEO/content/partners are Month 9+ is a **false dilemma**. It assumes only these channels exist. Warm network outreach was implicitly downgraded because it felt small or non-scalable. But **"small" and "slow" are not the same as "wrong."**

- Warm outreach is **faster** than all other channels
- Warm outreach is **more efficient** than cold outbound
- Warm outreach **creates the proof points** that make cold outbound work

The flaw in the original assumption: it treated "Year 1 pipeline" as a volume problem. It's not. It's a **trust initialization** problem. Cold outbound can't solve trust initialization. Only relationships can.
