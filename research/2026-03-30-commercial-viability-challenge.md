# Commercial Viability Challenge: The Commercial Co-Founder Dependency

**Auditor:** Commercial Viability Auditor
**Date:** 2026-03-30
**Challenge:** What if Louis CANNOT recruit a commercial co-founder in the next 6 months?

---

## 1. The Assumption Being Challenged

**Assumption:** Path A (senior commercial co-founder) is the only viable commercial path. Without one, Alizé cannot close €2,000+/month deals, and Louis's technical founder profile is a ceiling, not a floor (D84, D219).

**The contingency question not answered:** What happens if Path A fails? What is the plan if no commercial co-founder can be recruited in 3, 6, or 12 months?

---

## 2. Evidence From Existing Research

### Supporting the Assumption

- **D84:** "Louis cannot credibly close €7,500+ deals without senior commercial co-founder; profile is ceiling at that price point."
- **D219:** "Commercial model structurally broken — requires senior commercial co-founder OR downmarket repositioning OR channel partnership."
- **D74:** "Louis should NOT solo-close warm network deals — Grinto conflict of interest + no sales skills + LinkedIn DM wrong format."
- **D232:** Downmarket path (€500-800/month) killed — unit economics fail.
- **D224/D225:** Channel partner path dead — expert-comptables are ICP clients, not referral channels.

### Contradicting the Assumption

- **D247:** "Zero market data — all commercial model decisions D1-D242 made without a single sales attempt, outreach, discovery call, demo, or pilot."
- **D84 is a prediction, not evidence.** No outreach attempt has tested whether Louis can close at any price point. The ceiling was asserted, not measured.
- **D74's reasoning is specific to warm network + Grinto conflict.** It does not prove Louis cannot close in cold outbound or through a different relationship context.
- **No commercial co-founder candidate has been identified.** Path A has zero market validation — no prospect list, no conversations, no timeline.
- **The contingency for Path A failure is undefined.** Every other path (B and C) has been killed. The plan has no fallback.

---

## 3. Verdict

**The commercial co-founder dependency is a fatal planning flaw, not a confirmed commercial requirement.**

The reasoning:

**First**, D84 is a counterfactual prediction, not a validated constraint. "Louis cannot credibly close €7,500+" is an untested assumption that has never been put in front of a prospect. The research process produced this conclusion by argument, not by experiment. D247 confirms this explicitly: zero market data exists.

**Second**, the plan has no contingency for Path A failure. When D232 killed downmarket and D224 killed channel partners, Path A became the only remaining option by elimination — not by evidence. This is dangerous planning. A strategy with no viable fallback and no validation path is not a strategy; it is a hope.

**Third**, Louis has not attempted sales. The D74 conclusion — "no sales skills" — is a self-assessment without a test. The research process itself acknowledges this gap (D247). Louis has never run a discovery call, sent a cold outbound sequence, or presented a price to a prospect. The claim that he cannot is asserted, not demonstrated.

**The honest question the debate never asked:** What would it take to validate or invalidate the "Louis can't close" hypothesis before declaring a commercial co-founder as a hard prerequisite?

---

## 4. Recommended Actions

### Immediate (This Week)

1. **Test the assumption before recruiting.** Louis sends 5 cold outbound messages this week — not to close, but to measure response rate and conversation quality. This is the minimum viable experiment to invalidate or confirm D84. No co-founder search until this data exists.

2. **Define Path A failure criteria explicitly.** How long? How many conversations? What specific type of candidate? Write the failure threshold before starting recruitment, not after months of silence.

3. **Identify one concrete commercial co-founder candidate.** Not "a senior commercial person" — a specific name, role, and relationship. If no candidate exists in Louis's network, Path A is already failing and the contingency question must be answered now.

### If Path A Cannot Be Secured in 90 Days

4. **Activate the SDR + Louis model as a documented fallback.** Louis handles technical delivery and discovery; a hired SDR handles follow-up, scheduling, and closing support. This is not ideal but it is executable within 30 days and costs nothing until a deal closes.

5. **Treat the first paid engagement as the commercial experiment.** Not a pilot — a real €1,500-2,000 engagement with a real client who pays real money. If Louis cannot close that, the evidence is real. If he can, D84 is revised.

### The Meta-Recommendation

6. **Stop treating the commercial co-founder as a prerequisite to action.** The research process has been suspended for 42 pulses waiting for prerequisites that never arrive (domain, binary choice, network map). Path A has the same structure: it blocks action until a condition is met, and that condition has no timeline. Begin outreach in parallel with co-founder search. The worst outcome is six months of silence on both fronts.

---

**Bottom line:** The commercial co-founder dependency is structurally necessary only if D84 is correct and Louis has genuinely never sold anything to anyone. Neither fact has been established. The first action is to test the assumption, not recruit around it.
