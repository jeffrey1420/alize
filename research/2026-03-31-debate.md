## Agent 1: Inbound GTM Challenge

**Assumption challenged:** "Self-serve/inbound-first is the right primary GTM for Alizé in Month 1-3."

### The Challenge

**Self-serve requires a product that serves itself. You don't have one.**

Inbound converts strangers into trials into paying customers. That funnel requires a product that can be discovered, evaluated, and purchased without human intervention. Alizé has zero product. A landing page with a "get early access" form isn't self-serve — it's a waitlist collector that dies quietly because there's nothing to access. Every minute spent on "inbound-first" strategy is time spent building a vending machine for a product that doesn't exist.

**The "productized offer" framing is passive by design — it assumes buyers materialize.**

Productized offers solve a conversion problem, not a discovery problem. You can have the most compelling offer in the world and it doesn't matter if zero people see it. Inbound takes 6-18 months to build meaningful momentum even for experienced operators with SEO expertise and content teams. Louis has 60+ pulses of research and hasn't registered the domain. Building inbound infrastructure for a product that doesn't exist is like installing a world-class espresso machine in a kitchen you haven't built yet.

**Self-serve still requires the credibility infrastructure that only paying clients can build.**

Here's the catch-22: self-serve works when prospects trust you enough to buy without talking to anyone. Trust comes from case studies, testimonials, visible customer outcomes, social proof. That credibility stack requires clients — paying clients who got results. Zero clients means zero proof. Zero proof means your self-serve funnel leaks at every step because visitors have no reason to trust a stranger's productized offer. You're not ready for self-serve because self-serve requires the trust you haven't earned yet.

**The actual GTM for zero product, zero clients, zero proof is outbound and manual delivery.**

The uncomfortable truth: Louis needs to sell personally, deliver personally, and charge personally. Identify 20-30 real prospects. Reach out directly. Offer to solve their specific problem manually — for money. Use that feedback to build the actual product. Every "pulse" spent debating GTM strategy while avoiding actual sales conversations is time spent perfecting the map instead of exploring the territory.

**The research has become the avoidance.** Inbound-first is a comfortable fiction that delays the only thing that matters: getting a client, delivering value, and earning the proof that makes every future sale easier.
