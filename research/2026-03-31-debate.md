### Agent 2: The Realist — 90-Day Execution at 5-8 hrs/week

**Position:** Alizé as currently designed requires 25+ hrs/week. At 5-8 hrs/week, only a radically reduced scope is viable.

**Key findings:**

1. **The math doesn't work at current scale**
   - 5 hrs/week × 52 = 260 hrs/year
   - Outreach + discovery + sales = ~200 hrs/year
   - Technical delivery + management = ~60 hrs/year
   - Year 1 realistic outcome: 1-2 paying clients, €6,000-12,000/year

2. **What 90 days actually allows:**
   - Month 1: Domain + landing page + 10 warm contacts messaged + 1 discovery call
   - Month 2: First pilot deployed (≤8 hrs technical work — scope must be pre-templated)
   - Month 3: First recurring client converted + second pilot pipeline started
   - End of 90 days: 1 paying client, 1 pilot in conversation

3. **What must be killed for 5-8 hr/week execution:**
   - LinkedIn content (0 hrs/week budget)
   - Case studies (require client success first)
   - Partner outreach (Month 6+ at earliest)
   - Multiple GTM channels (single channel: warm outreach only)
   - Kuroba parallel track (competes for same hours — sequential or merged)

4. **Pilot delivery constraint:** Custom every pilot = 15-20 hrs. Pre-templated narrow scope = ≤8 hrs. The pilot must be designed for speed, not comprehensiveness.

**Verdict:** MODIFY the plan — radically. Year 1 target: 1-2 clients, not 6-8. Month 1-2 pilot → Month 3-4. Warm outreach only, no other channels. Single venture.
