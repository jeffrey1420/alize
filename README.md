## What We Do

We help businesses identify, deploy, and manage AI agents that handle repetitive, standardizable, or time-consuming tasks in their business processes.

## Services

- **Audit & Scoping** — Identifying high-impact use cases
- **Workflow Design** — Designing agent workflows around real needs
- **Technical Deployment** — Integration with your existing tools
- **Rights & Guardrails** — Configuring access controls and safety measures
- **Document Structuring / RAG** — Knowledge base setup
- **Business Testing** — Validating with real scenarios
- **Team Training** — Getting your teams on board
- **Maintenance & Monitoring** — Keeping things running smoothly
- **Support & Optimization** — Continuous improvement

## Priority Use Cases

- **Customer Service** — Request routing, simple Q&A, response drafting, knowledge base search
- **Sales** — Lead qualification, enrichment, follow-ups, meeting preparation, CRM updates
- **HR** — Internal Q&A, onboarding assistance, document search, candidate screening
- **Ops/Support** — Procedure tracking, internal request processing, document synthesis
- **Management/Admin** — Meeting notes, action tracking, document search, reporting
